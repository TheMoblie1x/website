Native vs Flutter vs React Native: How to Choose

By Rahul Pahuja, Founder · 2026-09-29

Choose native when you need the deepest platform capability or top performance on one platform. Choose Flutter or React Native when one codebase for Android and iOS matters more. The right answer depends on your product, team and roadmap.

The short answer

There is no universally best option. The decision comes down to what your app must do, how many platforms you need at launch, and who will maintain it afterwards.

Side by side

Native (Android / iOS)FlutterReact Native
CodebasesOne per platformOne sharedOne shared
Platform featuresFull and earliest accessThrough plugins and channelsThrough native modules
PerformanceBest ceilingStrong for most appsStrong for most apps
Best whenPerformance or platform APIs are centralCustom UI, both stores, one teamWeb-skilled team, both stores
Watch out forTwo codebases to maintainDependence on plugins for new platform featuresDependence on native modules for new platform features

Questions that decide it

  • Do you need both Android and iOS at launch?
  • Does the app rely on deep platform features such as background processing, sensors or on-device AI?
  • Who will maintain the code in two years?
  • How custom does the interface need to be?

Our approach

We work across native Android, iOS, Flutter and React Native, so the recommendation is not driven by a single toolset. We give you the reasoning in writing before the build begins, so you can challenge it.
