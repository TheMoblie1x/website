How to lock a note in Notes for Android

Protect a private note so it opens only with your biometrics.

Some notes are private. Locking a note keeps it behind your biometrics.

Before you start

Set up fingerprint or face unlock in your Android settings first. Notes uses your phone's biometrics to unlock protected notes.

Lock a note

  1. Open the note you want to protect.
  2. Tap the Lock icon in the top bar, next to Share and Reminder.
  3. Tap Save to keep the note.

How a locked note looks

On Home, a locked note shows as Protected Note, with the line "This note is encrypted and locked. Tap to unlock." Its contents stay hidden until you unlock it.

Open it later

  1. Tap the locked note on Home.
  2. Unlock with your biometrics.

Good to know

  • Lock only the notes that need it. Everything else stays quick to open.
  • Notes is offline-first, so your notes are kept on your device.
