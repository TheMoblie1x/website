How to turn a photo of handwriting into text in Notes for Android

Scan a page or whiteboard photo so the words become text you can search and edit.

Handwritten pages and whiteboards are hard to search. Scan fixes that: pick a photo, and Notes reads the text into the note.

Before you start

Scan opens your phone's photo picker, so take the photo first with your camera app. Make it a good one:

  • Use even light and avoid shadows across the page.
  • Hold the phone square to the page so the lines are straight.
  • Fill the frame with the writing, and keep the page flat.

Steps

  1. Tap the round + button on Home, then tap Scan. You can also open a note and tap Scan in the toolbar above the bottom tabs.
  2. Your photo picker opens. Choose the photo of the handwriting or whiteboard.
  3. Wait a moment for Notes to read the photo.
  4. Check the text, fix anything it misread, and tap Save.

After scanning

The text is part of the note, not a flat picture. You can edit it, add to it, and find it later with Search.

If a word comes out wrong

Neat, dark handwriting on plain paper reads best. If a page gives you trouble, retake the photo with better light and try again.
