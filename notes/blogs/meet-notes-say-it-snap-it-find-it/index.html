Meet Notes: say it, snap it, find it

What Notes is, who it is for, and the few ideas it is built on.

Most notes apps ask you to be tidy. Notes asks you to be quick.

It is an app for Android and iPhone from Mobile1X, built around one idea: getting a thought down should take seconds, and finding it again should take less.

Three ways in

  • Say it. Dictate a note in Hindi, English, or both in the same sentence.
  • Snap it. Photograph handwriting or a whiteboard, and Notes reads the text so you can search and edit it.
  • Type it. Plain notes with bold, italics and lists, for the times when typing is fastest.

It files things for you

Magic Sort categorizes your notes as you write them. You do not have to build a system of folders first, though you can make folders when you want them.

Your notes stay close

Notes is offline-first: your notes live on your device. Notes you want to keep private can be locked behind your biometrics.

What else is in there

Reminders, a favourite star, read-aloud, sharing, and actions to translate or summarize a note. The tour of Notes for Android shows where each one lives.

Get it

Notes is on Google Play and the App Store. If you are new, start with how to create your first note.
