Questions

Quick answers.

Which languages does voice notes support?

Hindi and English. Speak in either, or mix both in one sentence.

Is Notes on both Android and iPhone?

Yes. Get it on Google Play for Android or the App Store for iPhone.

Where are my notes stored?

On your phone. You can lock individual notes behind your biometrics.

Can I search my handwriting?

Yes. Photograph a page or whiteboard and the text becomes searchable and editable inside the note.

What is Magic Sort?

It categorizes your notes as you write them, so you don't have to build folders or remember tags.
