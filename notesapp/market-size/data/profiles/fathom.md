# Fathom

## Overview
- **Acquired by Superhuman**, announced September 14, 2026 (deal terms undisclosed). Prior to acquisition, Fathom was a private, VC-backed (and partly crowdfunded, via Wefunder) standalone company; it is now part of Superhuman's product suite. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/14/superhuman-acquires-yc-backed-notetaker-fathom-as-productivity-platforms-push-for-agentic-work/), [Superhuman blog](https://blog.superhuman.com/superhuman-acquires-fathom/).
- Founded in 2020; a Y Combinator company (YC W21 batch). Headquartered in the United States (San Francisco Bay Area).
- Employee count: not precisely disclosed; company was lean pre-acquisition given ~$30M total funding and rapid ARR growth relative to headcount (typical of AI-notetaker startups in this size class — estimated at roughly 30-60 people based on funding scale, not independently confirmed).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription (freemium), per-seat pricing, with a genuinely generous free tier that drove its large user base.
- Pricing (2026, pre-acquisition figures): Free (unlimited recordings/transcripts/storage across Zoom/Meet/Teams, but AI summaries capped at 5 calls/month); Premium (~$16/user/month billed annually, or ~$20/month, individual-only, uncaps AI summaries); Team Edition (~$15/user/month annual, or ~$19/month, 2-user minimum, adds shared search/playlists/collaboration); Business (~$25/user/month annual, or ~$34/month, 2-user minimum, adds native Salesforce/HubSpot sync, Deal View, AI scorecards). Source: [Fathom Pricing](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing).
- Target customer: individual professionals and sales teams; particularly strong in HubSpot-centric small-to-midsize sales organizations.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: high-quality meeting capture with "bot-free" recording options (avoiding the visible meeting-bot presence some competitors require), live in-meeting summaries, and a best-in-class free tier that drove over 400,000 monthly active users. Named HubSpot's 2025 Most Used App of the Year and recognized in G2's 2026 Best Software Awards Top 100.
- Platform availability: integrations with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams; deep CRM sync (HubSpot, Salesforce).
- Key features: live summaries as meetings unfold, action-item extraction, CRM field auto-sync, and (post-acquisition) planned integration into Superhuman's broader AI assistant "Superhuman Go," which operates across email, calendar, docs, and databases — repositioning Fathom's meeting intelligence as one input into a larger cross-app AI agent rather than a standalone product.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: reported to have reached $30M ARR in 2025, up from $10M in 2024 and $1M in 2023 — a roughly 90x revenue increase over two years per company/press reporting, with usage up ~20x over the same period. Source: [Yahoo Finance / TechCrunch reporting](https://www.yahoo.com/news/ai-notetaker-fathom-raises-17m-130000080.html).
- Users: over 400,000 monthly active users at time of acquisition; over 8,500 companies using its HubSpot integration specifically.
- Funding history: raised over $30M in total funding; most recent round was a $17M Series A in September 2024 led by Telescope Partners, which notably included $2M raised via Wefunder crowdfunding. Valued at $94M in that 2024 round (PitchBook). Acquired by Superhuman in September 2026 for an undisclosed sum.
- Growth trajectory: extremely fast — 90x revenue growth in two years pre-acquisition — which was very likely the primary driver behind Superhuman's acquisition interest, given the broader "agentic work" push across productivity platforms.

## Competitive Position
Fathom built one of the fastest-growing positions in the AI meeting-notetaker category through an unusually generous free tier and strong CRM integrations, culminating in its September 2026 acquisition by Superhuman — a consolidation move as productivity-suite players race to bundle meeting intelligence into broader "agentic" AI assistants rather than compete as standalone point solutions. As part of Superhuman, Fathom's future competitive position shifts from standing alone against Otter/Fireflies/Granola to serving as an embedded capability inside a multi-app AI assistant, which could be a stronger moat (bundling) or a dilution of its standalone brand, depending on integration execution.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Free/Premium/Team Edition/Business tiers, pre-acquisition pricing).
- **Secondary monetization:** Native CRM sync (Salesforce, HubSpot) and AI scorecards/Deal View bundled into the higher-priced Business tier — a sales-workflow upsell layer beyond core transcription/summarization; post-acquisition, Fathom's meeting intelligence is being folded into Superhuman's broader "Superhuman Go" AI assistant rather than monetized standalone.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — an unusually generous free tier (unlimited recordings/transcripts, capped AI summaries) plus "bot-free" recording drove viral adoption to 400,000+ monthly active users; minimal evidence of a heavy outbound sales motion pre-acquisition, consistent with lean YC-startup capital efficiency (~$30M total raised).
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $15–$60 (estimated, blended). Fathom's best-in-class free tier and product-led virality (90x revenue growth in two years on modest funding) suggest one of the lowest CAC profiles in the category, near the bottom of the meeting-AI freemium range.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $200–$500 (estimated, blended), assuming an average paying seat at ~$15-34/month effective price retained roughly 1.5-2 years — plausible given the fast-growing but still-young customer base (most growth occurred in 2024-2025) and strong HubSpot-ecosystem stickiness among its sales-team users.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated) — healthy, and likely the underlying economic story (fast, capital-efficient revenue growth from a low-CAC freemium funnel) that made Fathom an attractive acquisition target for Superhuman.
- **User demographics:** Individual professionals and sales teams, particularly HubSpot-centric small-to-midsize sales organizations (named HubSpot's 2025 Most Used App of the Year); primarily US-based, prosumer-to-SMB skew.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant, top countries: United States (~70%), Canada (~7%), United Kingdom (~6%), Australia (~4%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
