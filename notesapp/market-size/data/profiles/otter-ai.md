# Otter.ai

## Overview
- Private, VC-backed company; no parent (independent). Legal name Otter.ai, Inc.
- Founded in 2016 by Sam Liang and Yun Fu; headquartered in Mountain View / Los Altos, California.
- Employee count: approximately 200 people as of 2026 (estimated) — notably lean relative to revenue, with company messaging emphasizing over $500,000 in revenue per employee. Source: [getlatka.com](https://getlatka.com/companies/otter.ai).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription (freemium), per-seat pricing with monthly minute caps that expand at higher tiers.
- Pricing (2026): Basic/Free ($0, 300 minutes/month, 30-minute cap per conversation); Pro (~$16.99/user/month, or ~$8.33/month billed annually, 1,200 minutes, 90-minute cap); Business (~$30/user/month, or ~$19.99/month billed annually, no monthly cap, 4-hour meeting cap); Enterprise (custom pricing, SSO, SOC 2, API access, dedicated support). Source: [Claap — Otter Pricing](https://www.claap.io/blog/otter-pricing).
- Target customer: broad — individual professionals, sales/CS teams, and enterprise customers; increasingly pushing into enterprise with "AI meeting agents."

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: one of the earliest and most widely adopted standalone AI meeting-transcription tools, with strong brand recognition ("Otter" as a generic verb for meeting notes in many circles) and a large installed base predating the post-ChatGPT wave of AI-notetaker entrants.
- Platform availability: web, iOS/Android mobile apps, Chrome extension, integrations/bots for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams.
- Key features: real-time transcription, automated summaries, speaker identification, and in 2025 the launch of "AI meeting agents" — proactive AI that can join meetings, answer questions, and take actions, expanding beyond passive transcription into agentic assistance.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: reported to have crossed $100M ARR in March 2025, up from $81M ARR at the end of 2024 — reported directly by the company. Source: [Otter.ai blog — "Caps Transformational 2025 with $100M ARR Milestone"](https://otter.ai/blog/otter-ai-caps-transformational-2025-with-100m-arr-milestone-industry-first-ai-meeting-agents-and-global-enterprise-expansion).
- Users: over 25 million users reported as of 2025 (company-reported/estimated).
- Funding history: raised approximately $70M in total funding since 2016; sources disagree on the most recent disclosed valuation (one aggregator lists a low figure of ~$66.9M, which appears to reflect an early/stale funding-round valuation rather than the company's current implied value given $100M ARR — treat any specific valuation figure here as low-confidence and likely outdated).
- Growth trajectory: strong, accelerating ARR growth (~23% in Q1 2025 alone versus year-end 2024) driven by enterprise expansion and the shift from passive transcription to "AI meeting agents."

## Competitive Position
Otter.ai is one of the incumbent leaders in the AI meeting-notetaker category, with a large existing user base and strong brand recognition that predates the 2023-2025 wave of AI-notetaker startups (Fireflies, Fathom, Granola, etc.). Its moat is scale and enterprise trust built over nearly a decade, but the category has become intensely competitive and commoditized, with well-funded rivals (Fireflies at unicorn status, Fathom acquired by Superhuman) pushing free tiers and CRM-native integrations that pressure Otter's pricing power and differentiation.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription with usage-based minute caps (Basic/Pro/Business tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** Enterprise tier (custom pricing, SSO/SOC 2/API access) and the 2025-launched "AI meeting agents" — a premium, proactive-AI upsell layered on top of passive transcription that is central to Otter's push into enterprise expansion.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — a generous free tier (300 min/month) built a large individual-user base over nearly a decade of brand recognition ("Otter" as a verb for meeting notes), with a growing inside-sales motion layered on top to convert teams into Business/Enterprise plans.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $30–$100 (estimated, blended). As an incumbent with strong organic brand pull and freemium virality, Otter's CAC likely sits mid-range for meeting-AI consumer/prosumer tools — higher than newer, more viral entrants like Fathom, but still well below sales-led B2B SaaS.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $200–$500 (estimated, blended across Pro/Business tiers), assuming an average paying seat at ~$10-20/month effective price retained roughly 2 years, with enterprise seats pulling the upper end higher given Otter's growing enterprise mix and 25M+ user base.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~5:1 (estimated) — healthy, consistent with Otter's reported accelerating ARR growth (crossing $100M ARR in March 2025) and enterprise expansion strategy.
- **User demographics:** Broad — individual professionals, sales/CS teams, and increasingly enterprise buyers; skews toward US/English-speaking knowledge workers given its long incumbency in the US meeting-AI market.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant, top countries: United States (~65%), Canada (~7%), United Kingdom (~6%), Australia (~4%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
