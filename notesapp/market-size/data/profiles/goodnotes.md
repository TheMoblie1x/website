# Goodnotes

## Overview
- Ownership: Privately held, venture-backed but historically profitable/bootstrapped-leaning. Hong Kong-based Goodnotes.com Limited (previously operating as Time Base Technology Limited).
- Founded: 2011 (app launched 2011; company incorporated in Hong Kong) by Steven Chan.
- HQ: Hong Kong / Kowloon, with a growing UK/London presence for its AI and international teams.
- Employees: Estimated 150-250 (public sources cite Time Base Technology/Goodnotes growing from a two-person team to over 150 staff by the early 2020s; grown further with 2024-2025 AI investments).

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription plus legacy one-time purchase (Special Edition), transitioned from a pure one-time-purchase app (pre-2022) to tiered subscriptions.
- Pricing (per support.goodnotes.com, 2026): Free tier (3-document limit); Essential at $11.99/year; Pro at $35.99/year; Special Edition (Apple-only) one-time purchase at $35.99; optional AI Pass add-on at roughly $10/month for full AI features.
- Target customer: Students (core historical base), plus expanding into professionals, educators, and general productivity users on iPad/tablet and increasingly cross-platform (Android, Windows, Web).

## Product & Positioning
- Positioned as the premium, design-polished digital paper/handwriting app, historically strongest on iPad with Apple Pencil.
- Platforms: iOS/iPadOS, macOS, Android, Windows, and Web — expanded from Apple-exclusive to full cross-platform by 2023-2024.
- Differentiators: handwriting-to-text conversion, PDF annotation, templates/planner ecosystem, AI features (Goodnotes AI: math assist, summarization, "Create" note generation), and a large third-party template/sticker marketplace.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: Not publicly disclosed with audited figures; third-party estimates (Sensor Tower-style app revenue trackers) have placed Goodnotes among the highest-grossing productivity apps on iOS, with estimated annual revenue in the tens of millions of dollars (specific figure not confirmed by primary source, so treated as estimated).
- Users: Company states over 30 million registered users globally (per goodnotes.com/about-us and press materials).
- Funding: Reported to have raised a $6 million seed round from Race Capital (2020). Subsequently reported to have received growth investment involving Sequoia China / HongShan (the China-focused successor to Sequoia Capital China), though exact amount and valuation are not fully disclosed in public filings; treat any specific valuation figure as unconfirmed/estimated pending primary disclosure.
- Growth trajectory: Company has publicly described itself as profitable pre-funding, then raised outside capital to accelerate AI product development and its 2024 acquisition of an AI video-summary startup (reported by TechCrunch, Jan 2024), signaling a pivot beyond pure education/student use cases toward broader knowledge-work AI features.

## Competitive Position
Goodnotes is the category leader by brand recognition and revenue among iPad-first handwriting apps, with a strong design/UX moat, large user base, and now AI investment to defend against Notability and newer AI-native note apps. Its moat is more brand/ecosystem (templates, community, cross-platform parity) than pure technology, since core handwriting/PDF annotation features are increasingly commoditized. Continued success depends on successfully monetizing AI add-ons without alienating its price-sensitive student base.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (annual Essential/Pro tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** AI Pass add-on (~$10/month), legacy Special Edition one-time purchase (Apple-only), third-party template/sticker marketplace.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — App Store search/browse, student word-of-mouth, and a free 3-document tier that converts into paid Essential/Pro subscriptions, supplemented by some brand/content marketing around AI features.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $15–$55 (estimated). Sits at the higher end of the indie freemium consumer-app band given Goodnotes' scale, cross-platform expansion spend, and growing brand/content marketing investment since its 2024 AI push.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $55–$190 (estimated), based on the $35.99/year Pro price point retained for roughly 1.5–5 years (students churn faster; professional/AI-adopting users retain longer), with AI Pass add-on revenue pushing the top of the range up.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~3.5:1 (estimated). Healthy and above the 3:1 sustainability bar, consistent with a category leader with strong organic acquisition.
- **User demographics:** Primarily students (high school/university), 16–30, plus a growing share of knowledge workers/professionals; iPad/Apple Pencil-heavy historically, now broadening across Android/Windows/Web. Global reach with likely concentration in the US, UK, and East Asia (per company's ~30M registered users claim); specific demographic splits are inferred from positioning and platform mix, not disclosed.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global/Distributed, top countries: United States (~25%), Japan (~13%), United Kingdom (~10%) — estimated from HQ location (Hong Kong, with growing UK presence) / platform reach (iPad/Apple Pencil-heavy, ~30M registered users, broad international App Store presence), not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
