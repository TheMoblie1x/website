# tl;dv

## Overview
- Independent, privately held, VC-backed startup (not a subsidiary of a larger platform).
- Founded 2020 by Raphael Allstadt, Carlo Thissen, and Allan Bettarel; headquartered in Aachen, Germany (with distributed/remote team).
- Employee count estimate: roughly 100-150 (estimated based on typical headcount for a startup at this funding/revenue stage with 2M+ users; not disclosed).

## Business Model
- Freemium SaaS: a generous free tier drives adoption, paid tiers monetize sales/revenue teams and larger orgs.
- Pricing (per user/month, billed annually unless noted): Free (unlimited recording/transcription, AI summaries); Pro at $18/mo annual ($22 monthly); Business at $59/mo annual, which adds AI sales coaching, cross-meeting analytics, and CRM sync (HubSpot, Salesforce). Source: claap.io/blog/tl-dv-pricing, get-alfred.ai/blog/tldv-pricing.
- Target customer: initially broad knowledge-worker/productivity users, now leaning into sales and revenue teams via the Business tier's coaching and CRM features.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiators: botless/native recording options, AI Agents for meetings, "Multi-Meeting Intelligence" that surfaces insights across hundreds of calls, and sales-coaching that scores calls against a playbook.
- Platform availability: browser extension and integrations with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams; claims 5,000+ integrations; transcription in 40+ languages via OpenAI Whisper.
- Key features defining competitive position: moving up-market from a lightweight "highlight clips" notetaker toward a revenue-intelligence platform that competes with Gong/Chorus-style tools at a much lower price point.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed; estimated in the low tens of millions of dollars ARR based on a 2M+ user base and freemium-to-paid conversion norms for the category (estimated).
- Users: over 2 million users reported (tl;dv marketing materials, aggregated via search results).
- Funding: reported figures vary by source — Crunchbase-adjacent trackers cite $8M total raised (Series A-stage) while other trackers cite $4.49M across 1 round from 6 investors; exact syndicate not confirmed in available sources. Treat funding total as approximate/estimated pending a primary-source filing.
- Growth trajectory: expanding from a notetaking tool into an "AI Agents for meetings" and sales-coaching platform through 2026, signaling a push up-market against better-funded incumbents.

## Competitive Position
- tl;dv sits in the crowded AI-notetaker middle market, differentiated by aggressive freemium pricing and a genuine push into sales coaching/CRM territory typically owned by Gong and Chorus.
- Its moat is thin — the underlying transcription/summarization layer is largely commoditized (Whisper-based) — so its edge depends on continued feature velocity (multi-meeting intelligence, agents) and price/value positioning rather than proprietary technology.
- Facing intensifying competition from Granola, Fathom (now owned by Superhuman), Zoom AI Companion (bundled free), and Read AI, all iterating rapidly in the same space.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Pro/Business tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** Upsell from free to Pro, and from Pro to the higher-priced Business tier for sales-coaching/CRM sync features; no ads.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — generous free tier drives viral/organic signups, with an inside-sales layer added for Business-tier deals targeting revenue teams.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $40–$150 (estimated) — mostly PLG/organic with some paid marketing and a light sales-assist motion for Business-tier accounts; freemium keeps blended CAC low relative to pure outbound SaaS.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $250–$700 (estimated) — assuming ~$18-22/mo blended ARPU with ~18-24 month average retention typical of SMB-heavy freemium meeting tools.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** roughly 4:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** General knowledge workers and sales/CS teams at SMB-to-mid-market companies; skews toward Europe (Aachen HQ) and North America; 2M+ registered users.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America and Europe, with a notably stronger Europe skew than typical for this category, top countries: United States (~32%), Germany (~16%), United Kingdom (~12%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
