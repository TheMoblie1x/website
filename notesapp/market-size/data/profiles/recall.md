# Recall

## Overview
- Private, VC-backed startup — product at getrecall.ai (branded "Recall," also seen at recall.it), distinct from the separately-run developer API company "Recall.ai" (meeting-bot/transcription API); this profile covers the consumer personal-knowledge app.
- Founded in 2022 in Amsterdam by Paul Richards (a South African fullstack developer with a background in knowledge graphs in cybersecurity); founding team also includes CTO Igor Gligorevic and COO Sankari Nair.
- Employee count estimate: very small, likely under 15 (estimated based on pre-seed funding size of $1.5M and startup stage).

## Business Model
- Freemium consumer SaaS: an AI-powered personal knowledge base/encyclopedia that summarizes, categorizes, and connects saved content (articles, videos, PDFs) into a personal knowledge graph, with free and paid subscription tiers (per recall.it/pricing — "Free, Plus & Max" plans; specific $ figures not consistently confirmed across sources and should be verified directly on recall.it).
- Target customer: individual knowledge workers, students, and lifelong learners wanting to retain and resurface information they consume online, using spaced-repetition/active-recall learning techniques.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: applies cognitive-science learning techniques (active recall, spaced repetition) to AI-curated content rather than just passive storage — explicitly framed as "AI gave everyone the same brain, Recall gives AI yours," emphasizing personalization of the knowledge graph.
- Platform availability: Web app, iOS, Android, and browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox (for clipping/saving content while browsing).
- Key features: automatic summarization and categorization of saved content, AI-built personal knowledge graph, spaced-repetition review/flashcard-style resurfacing, cross-platform saving via browser extension and mobile apps.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed.
- Adoption proxy: reported 500,000+ users across web, iOS, Android, Chrome, and Firefox — a meaningful adoption signal for a startup at this funding stage.
- Funding history: $1.5M pre-seed round, led by Jason Calacanis, with participation from Blockchain Founders Capital, Rocket Capital, and an initial investment from Splash Capital that came within 8 hours of a viral Hacker News launch post.
- Growth trajectory: the Hacker-News-to-funding narrative and 500K+ user base suggest strong organic/viral growth relative to its small funding base, though later-stage funding or revenue scale-up has not been publicly confirmed.

## Competitive Position
Recall competes in the crowded "AI second brain" / read-it-later-plus-AI category (alongside Fabric, Mem, Notion AI features, and various AI note apps), differentiating through its learning-science angle (spaced repetition) rather than pure storage/search. Its moat is modest — the underlying summarization/knowledge-graph technology is increasingly commoditized by foundation-model APIs — so its defensibility rests on user habit formation (the review/recall loop) and its browser-extension distribution advantage rather than proprietary technology.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription — freemium consumer app with Free, Plus (~$10/month), and Max (~$38/month) tiers.
- **Secondary monetization:** Power-user Max tier upsell (bulk actions, frontier AI models) and a student discount program; no ads, no data/API licensing disclosed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — the product's 500K+ user base traces to a viral Hacker News launch and continued organic/browser-extension-driven acquisition rather than paid growth, consistent with its small $1.5M pre-seed round.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $10-30 (estimated) — very small pre-seed funding with a proven viral/organic growth motion (HN launch, browser extension virality) means little paid acquisition spend to date.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $100-200 (estimated) — based on the ~$10/month Plus tier at an assumed ~12-18 month average paid retention typical of a consumer productivity subscription.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Individual knowledge workers, students, and lifelong learners, roughly 20-35, skewing toward English-speaking, tech-savvy early adopters.
- **Estimated user geography:** Anglosphere-skewed consumer AI market, top countries: United States (~40%), United Kingdom (~15%), Netherlands (~8%), Canada (~7%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
