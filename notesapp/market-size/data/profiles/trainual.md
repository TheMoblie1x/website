# Trainual

## Overview
- Private company, VC-backed; no parent company.
- Founded by Chris Ronzio and Jonathan Ronzio (sources conflict on year — either 2014 or 2018; 2018 is more commonly cited as the company's formal launch). HQ in Scottsdale/Phoenix, Arizona.
- Employee count estimate: approximately 96-130 employees (sources conflict: PitchBook cites ~130, another source cites 96 as of December 2024).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription, flat-rate tiered pricing that includes a set number of employees rather than pure per-seat pricing.
- Pricing tiers (as of 2026, cited from Trainual/aggregator sources): Train tier at $199/month (up to 50 employees), Scale tier at $299/month (up to 100 employees), Grow tier custom-priced for larger teams.
- Target customer: Small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs) needing to document processes, onboard employees, and scale operations/training without a dedicated L&D team.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: Positions itself as an "operations manual" / SOP and training documentation platform specifically built for SMBs scaling headcount, rather than a general wiki or enterprise LMS.
- Platform availability: Web app with mobile apps (iOS/Android) for on-the-go training access.
- Key features: Process/SOP documentation templates, built-in training assignments with completion tracking, org chart and role documentation, quizzes for knowledge verification, and integrations with HR/payroll tools (e.g., Gusto).

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: Not publicly disclosed; no reliable ARR figure found in available sources.
- Customers: Not disclosed in available sources; historically marketed as serving thousands of small businesses (unverified for current period).
- Funding history: Raised approximately $33.8M-$34M total, with investors including MATH Venture Partners, Altos Ventures Management, and 4490 Ventures (source: Tracxn/PitchBook aggregation).
- Growth trajectory: No recent (2025-2026) funding rounds or major growth announcements found, suggesting the company may be in a steady-state or bootstrapped-growth phase following its last disclosed raises.

## Competitive Position
Trainual occupies a defensible niche as an SMB-focused SOP/training documentation tool, differentiated from general wikis (Outline, GitBook) by its structured onboarding/training workflows and from enterprise LMS platforms by its simplicity and SMB pricing. Its moat is moderate — the category (SMB process documentation + training) has low switching costs and faces competitive pressure from both broader wiki tools adding training features and dedicated LMS vendors moving downmarket.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Core/Pro/Premium/Enterprise tiers, billed annually)
- **Secondary monetization:** Optional compliance/training course content add-ons; one-time implementation/onboarding fee (~$1,000 baseline); no ads
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — transparent flat-rate entry pricing for small teams (~$249/mo for 10 seats) converts to custom, sales-assisted pricing above 10 users.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $2,500–$8,000 (estimated) — hybrid SMB self-serve-to-inside-sales motion; higher than pure dev-tool PLG due to onboarding-heavy sale (training content setup) but well below full enterprise sales-led CAC.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $9,000–$20,000 (estimated) — assumes ~$3,000–$5,000 average annual contract value (small-team seat count) and a ~3–4 year customer lifetime.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~3:1 (estimated)
- **User demographics:** Small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs), primarily non-tech (franchises, healthcare, trades, agencies); ops/HR/L&D leaders are the buyers; US-centric customer base.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America, top countries: United States (~85%), Canada (~6%), Australia (~3%) — estimated from HQ location (Phoenix, AZ) and SMB customer base, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
