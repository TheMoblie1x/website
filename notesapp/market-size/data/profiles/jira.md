# Jira (Atlassian) — adjacent to Confluence-style team knowledge

## Overview
- Public subsidiary product: Jira is a core product of Atlassian Corporation (NASDAQ: TEAM), a public company. Note: this profile treats Jira briefly, only as it relates to Confluence-adjacent team knowledge workflows (e.g., linking issues to Confluence docs, Jira as a system of record alongside Confluence as the system of knowledge) — not as a full Jira/project-management profile.
- Atlassian founded 2002 in Sydney, Australia (Mike Cannon-Brookes, Scott Farquhar); dual-HQ in Sydney and San Francisco.
- Employee count: approximately 13,813 (2025, company disclosure).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription (per-user pricing), with Jira sold standalone or bundled with Confluence and other Atlassian products (Jira Service Management, etc.) in suite deals; also legacy Data Center (self-hosted) offering for large enterprises, being phased toward cloud.
- Pricing: Jira Software Standard around $8.15/user/month (annual billing); Atlassian raised prices in October 2025 (~5% Standard, ~7.5% Premium, up to ~10% Enterprise). Confluence (the adjacent knowledge product) is commonly purchased alongside Jira by the same engineering/product teams for linked documentation.
- Target customer: software development and IT teams needing issue tracking/agile project management, frequently paired with Confluence for specs, retros, and knowledge base content that references Jira tickets directly.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator (in this adjacent context): deep two-way linking between Jira issues and Confluence pages, letting engineering teams keep "what we're building" (Jira) and "why/how we're building it" (Confluence) connected — a workflow moat that pure-play wiki or project-management tools lack individually.
- Platform availability: cloud SaaS (primary push), Data Center (self-hosted, enterprise, being sunset in favor of cloud), mobile apps.
- Key features relevant to knowledge workflows: issue-to-page linking, Confluence "Jira macro" embeds (live issue lists in wiki pages), unified Atlassian Intelligence (AI) features spanning both products, shared admin/identity across the suite.

## Financial Profile
- Revenue: Atlassian total company revenue $5.22B (FY2025); Jira-specific revenue not broken out separately in filings (bundled into "Software" segment revenue).
- Adoption: Atlassian reports 125K+ Jira customers and 100K+ Confluence customers as of Q4 FY25, with over 300K total customers and 500+ customers spending $1M+ annually — illustrating substantial customer overlap between the two products.
- Funding: N/A — public company since December 2015 IPO (NASDAQ: TEAM); no longer privately funded.
- Growth trajectory: continued double-digit-adjacent revenue growth (~21% YoY cited in a recent quarter), driven by cloud migration and price increases, with the Jira+Confluence combination remaining a core cross-sell motion.

## Competitive Position
Within the team-knowledge space, Jira's relevance is as Confluence's natural pairing product — the integrated Jira+Confluence workflow is a meaningful retention lever against standalone wiki competitors (Notion, XWiki, BookStack) that cannot offer native, bidirectional issue-tracking integration at the same depth. Atlassian's moat here is ecosystem lock-in and cross-product workflow integration rather than either product being best-in-class individually, and this bundling pressure is precisely what open-source alternatives like XWiki target with dedicated "Confluence migration" tooling.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** SaaS subscription (per-user pricing), sold standalone or bundled with Confluence and other Atlassian suite products.
- **Secondary monetization:** Legacy Data Center (self-hosted, enterprise) licenses being phased out toward cloud; suite bundling with Jira Service Management and Atlassian Intelligence AI add-ons; cross-sell into Confluence for knowledge-adjacent workflows.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — free/low-cost entry tiers drive bottoms-up team adoption, with inside/enterprise sales taking over for larger suite deals (300K+ total customers, 500+ spending $1M+ annually).
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** ~$0 standalone CAC in the context of this profile — Jira is treated here only as adjacent to Confluence-style team knowledge workflows, i.e., a bundled/cross-sell feature of Atlassian's much larger suite rather than an independently acquired product; true company-wide Jira CAC is not broken out in filings.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** Not meaningfully separable at the feature level for this profile's scope; Atlassian's overall scale ($5.22B FY2025 revenue, 125K+ Jira customers) implies high blended per-account LTV, but Jira-specific LTV is undisclosed.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** N/A — out of scope as a standalone metric; Jira is bundled/adjacent within Atlassian's broader suite economics.
- **User demographics:** Software development and IT teams needing issue tracking/agile project management, frequently the same teams (engineering/product) that also use Confluence; global enterprise and SMB customer base, skewing toward technical/engineering organizations.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global/Distributed, top countries: United States (~35%), United Kingdom (~8%), Australia (~6%), Germany (~6%), India (~6%) — estimated from Atlassian's disclosed 300K+ global customer base and dual US/Australia HQ footprint, not disclosed.
