# NotebookLM (Google)

## Overview
- Product of Google, a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. (public company, NASDAQ: GOOGL). Not a standalone company.
- Launched publicly in 2023 out of Google Labs; developed by Google's internal teams. As of July 2026, Google rebranded the product to "Gemini Notebook" while continuing to fold it more tightly into the broader Gemini app ecosystem (Notebooks-in-Gemini feature launched April 2026).
- Employee count: not separately disclosed (part of Google's broader AI/Labs organization); Alphabet overall employs roughly 180,000+ people company-wide (parent-company figure, not specific to this product team).

## Business Model
- Bundled/free-to-use product tied to Google account tiers, monetized indirectly via Google Workspace/Gemini subscription upsells rather than standalone pricing.
- Pricing: free for individual Google account users with usage limits; expanded features and higher usage caps available to Google One AI Premium and Google Workspace/Gemini Enterprise subscribers (no separate NotebookLM-specific price tag; folded into existing Google subscription tiers). Source: blog.google product announcements, docs.cloud.google.com Gemini Enterprise release notes.
- Target customer: originally researchers, students, and knowledge workers doing source-grounded research/synthesis; expanding toward broader "project workspace" use cases for general Gemini users and enterprise Gemini Enterprise customers.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiators: source-grounded AI (answers and outputs are tied directly to user-uploaded documents/sources, reducing hallucination risk) and its widely popular "Audio Overview" (AI-generated podcast-style discussion of source material).
- Platform availability: web app, with growing integration directly into the Gemini app (Notebooks-in-Gemini) and mobile access via Google's broader app ecosystem.
- Key features: as of mid-2026, runs on Gemini 3.5, offers agentic source discovery within chat, executes code in a secure cloud sandbox for data analysis, and can output charts, PDFs, spreadsheets, structured data, and PowerPoint files — moving well beyond its original "notebook summarizer" scope.

- Growth trajectory: rapid feature expansion and rebranding (NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook, July 2026) alongside deep integration into the flagship Gemini app signals Google is elevating the product from a standalone research tool into a core pillar of its consumer and enterprise AI strategy.

## Competitive Position
- NotebookLM/Gemini Notebook is not a direct meeting-notetaking competitor to the other seven companies in this set (tl;dv, Avoma, Grain, Supernormal, Granola, Read AI, Zoom AI Companion) — its core use case is source-grounded document research and synthesis, though its trajectory (agentic capabilities, structured outputs) is converging with the broader "AI workspace assistant" category.
- Its moat is substantial: free distribution via Google's massive user base, deep integration with Gemini's frontier models, and a 30M+ user head start give it structural advantages no standalone startup can match on cost or reach.
- Its main constraint is organizational focus — as a feature within Alphabet's sprawling AI portfolio (competing internally for attention with Gemini app, Workspace AI, and Search AI features), its roadmap depends on continued internal prioritization rather than the singular focus a standalone startup can apply.
