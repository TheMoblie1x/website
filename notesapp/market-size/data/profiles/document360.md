# Document360

## Overview
- Product of Kovai.co, a privately held, fully bootstrapped (no venture capital) software company.
- Kovai.co founded by Saravana Kumar (founding dates cited variously as 2009 or 2011, depending on source); Document360 itself launched in 2018 out of an internal hackathon. Headquartered in Coimbatore ("Kovai"), Tamil Nadu, India, with additional international presence.
- Employee count estimate: Kovai.co (across all its products, of which Document360 is the flagship) has approximately 290 employees.

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription, quote-based/sales-assisted pricing rather than fixed public per-seat tiers — Document360 moved to fully custom, contact-sales pricing around November 2024, discontinuing its free tier and public price list.
- Pricing: historically had public tiers (Professional, Business, Enterprise) with legacy reference points around $149-199/month (Professional, ~2 users), ~$249-299/month (Business, ~5 users), and up to ~$499/month (Enterprise) per project — now entirely custom-quoted, with AI module ("Eddy AI") typically adding an estimated 30-50% to base subscription cost. (Source: aggregated via eesel.ai, happysupport.ai; treat legacy figures as historical reference, not current list price.)
- Target customer: SMB through enterprise, with strong focus on technical/product documentation and customer-facing knowledge bases (as opposed to purely internal wikis) — used across technology, healthcare, manufacturing, and government sectors.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: purpose-built for public and private knowledge base/documentation publishing (not just internal team wikis) — strong versioning, category management, and a polished public-facing help-center rendering layer, positioning it against Confluence and dedicated docs tools like GitBook.
- Platform availability: web-based authoring app with published knowledge base sites (custom domains), API documentation tooling, and AI features (Eddy AI) for content assistance and end-user search.
- Key features: article versioning/rollback, category manager, analytics on article performance/search gaps, localization/translation support, and both internal (private) and external (public) knowledge base modes.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Kovai.co (parent, all products combined) reported approximately $32.5M in annual recurring revenue (2024), entirely bootstrapped without outside funding.
- Document360 specifically has crossed $10M ARR and is targeting $25M ARR by 2028, per company statements reported in press coverage (YourStory, December 2025/2026 reporting window).
- Customer counts: Document360 cites 1,500+ customers across sectors as of recent company disclosures.
- Funding history: none — Kovai.co/Document360 has never raised institutional venture funding, an unusual and notable profile for a company at this revenue scale.
- Growth trajectory: steady, profitable growth from bootstrapped operations; the stated $10M→$25M ARR target by 2028 implies continued moderate double-digit-percentage annual growth expectations.

## Competitive Position
Document360 has carved out a credible, profitable position as a specialist in public/customer-facing knowledge base and technical documentation publishing, differentiated from generalist internal wikis (Confluence, Notion, Slite) by its focus on external help-center use cases, versioning rigor, and localization. Its bootstrapped status is both a strength (capital discipline, profitability) and a constraint (slower ability to out-invest VC-backed rivals in AI/enterprise features), but its ARR growth trajectory and lack of debt/dilution give it a durable, low-risk competitive footing in its documentation-focused niche.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Quote-based enterprise/sales-assisted SaaS contract — Document360 moved from public per-tier pricing to fully custom quotes in November 2024.
- **Secondary monetization:** Eddy AI module add-on (estimated +30–50% on base subscription), likely onboarding/professional services for larger deployments, and localization/translation add-ons.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Sales-led enterprise (increasingly) — the shift to quote-based pricing signals a deliberate move toward a sales-assisted motion, even though a large share of its base remains SMB-sized.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $2,500–$8,000 (estimated) — a quote-based, sales-assisted motion despite historically PLG-ish public pricing; likely kept disciplined relative to VC-backed peers given Kovai.co's fully bootstrapped, profit-focused operating model.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $10,000–$30,000 (estimated) — assuming ACV historically in the ~$1,800–$6,000+/year range (legacy public tiers) now trending higher with custom pricing and Eddy AI attach, over a 5–6 year average lifetime given the high migration friction of moving a public/customer-facing knowledge base to a competitor.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~4:1 (estimated) — healthy, consistent with a profitable, self-funded operator that must fund its own growth without outside capital.
- **User demographics:** SMB through enterprise, with the largest concentration still in smaller company bands (64% SMB per reviewer data) but a growing enterprise mix (13%); primary buyers are technical writers, product teams, and customer support/success functions; industries include SaaS, IT services, healthcare, and finance; US-heavy (~62%), with UK and India presence reflecting the company's Indian HQ.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-led customer base despite Indian HQ, top countries: United States (~62%), United Kingdom (~12%), India (~8%), Germany (~5%) — estimated from disclosed ~62% US customer share and Kovai.co's Indian HQ, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
