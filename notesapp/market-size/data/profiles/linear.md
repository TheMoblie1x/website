# Linear

## Overview
- Private, venture-backed company (not owned by a parent corporation).
- Founded in 2019 by Karri Saarinen (CEO), Tuomas Artman, and Jori Lallo; headquartered in San Francisco, California. All three founders are Finnish, and the product's minimalist design philosophy is frequently traced to that background.
- Employee count estimates vary by source: roughly 118–280 people as of 2025-2026 depending on the tracker (PitchBook/Latka-style estimates cluster around 120-180; some sources cite ~280). Company has stayed deliberately lean relative to revenue.

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription, per-seat pricing, billed monthly or annually (annual required for lower per-seat rates on paid tiers).
- Pricing tiers (per user/month, 2026): Free ($0, unlimited members, 2 teams, 250 issues), Basic (~$10), Business (~$16, adds unlimited teams, private teams, guest access, Linear Agent AI automation), Enterprise (custom, SSO/audit logs/dedicated support/SLAs). Source: [Linear Pricing 2026 — aiproductivity.ai](https://aiproductivity.ai/blog/linear-pricing/), [Quackback](https://quackback.io/blog/linear-pricing).
- Target customer: software engineering and product teams at startups and scale-ups; increasingly courting larger/enterprise engineering orgs via its Business and Enterprise tiers.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator is speed and design craft — a keyboard-first, low-latency issue tracker built as an alternative to Jira's perceived bloat and slowness, explicitly targeting "high-performance" software teams.
- Platform availability: web app, native macOS/Windows desktop apps, iOS/Android mobile apps; deep integrations with GitHub/GitLab, Slack, Figma, Sentry, and others.
- Key features: cycles (sprints), triage, roadmaps/projects, Linear Insights, and "Linear Agent," an AI-driven triage/automation layer added to the Business tier — positioning Linear increasingly as an agentic workflow tool, not just a tracker.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: reported around $100M ARR (estimated/reported via Latka-style aggregator); Linear has stated it has been cash-flow positive since 2021. Source: [Linear App Revenue — getlatka.com](https://getlatka.com/companies/linear.app).
- Customers: approximately 40,000 organizations reportedly use Linear (estimated, aggregator-sourced).
- Funding history: raised an $82M Series C in June 2025 at a $1.25B valuation, led by Accel with Sequoia Capital, 01 Advisors, Seven Seven Six, Designer Fund, and Index Ventures participating. Total funding to date is roughly $134M — notably small relative to valuation, reflecting the company's profitability and capital efficiency. Source: [Sacra — Linear](https://sacra.com/c/linear/), [PitchBook — Linear](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/343139-23).
- Growth trajectory: company messaging emphasizes profitability-led growth (holds more cash than total capital raised) rather than growth-at-all-costs, a deliberate contrast to Jira/Atlassian's enterprise sales motion.

## Competitive Position
Linear occupies a premium, design-led niche in developer/product-team issue tracking, competing most directly against Atlassian's Jira and, at the low end, tools like ClickUp and Shortcut. Its moat is workflow lock-in and brand loyalty among engineering teams who self-select for craft and speed, plus a capital-efficient, profitable business that reduces pressure to compromise on product quality. The risk is a ceiling: Linear's opinionated, narrow focus makes enterprise-wide expansion (beyond engineering) harder than for horizontal platforms like Atlassian or Monday.com.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Basic/Business tiers, billed monthly or annually).
- **Secondary monetization:** Enterprise tier (custom pricing, SSO/audit logs/SLAs) and Linear Agent, an AI-driven triage/automation add-on bundled into the Business tier — an emerging AI-feature upsell lever rather than a separate line item today.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — bottom-up adoption by individual engineering teams (aided by the generous free tier) that expands into paid Business/Enterprise via light-touch sales once usage crosses team size thresholds.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $300–$900 (estimated). Linear relies heavily on organic/product-led growth and word-of-mouth in developer circles, keeping CAC toward the low end of the self-serve dev-tool range; the top end reflects Enterprise-tier deals that require sales-assisted onboarding.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $3,000–$9,000 (estimated), assuming a typical paying team of ~8–15 seats at ~$16/seat/month (~$1,500–$2,900 ACV) retained roughly 3–4 years given Linear's high reported workflow lock-in and profitability-driven low churn.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~7:1 (estimated) — a healthy ratio consistent with Linear's stated cash-flow-positive status and capital-efficient growth strategy.
- **User demographics:** Software product/engineering teams at startups and scale-ups; skews toward design-conscious, craft-focused technical teams globally, with growing enterprise engineering-org adoption via the Business/Enterprise tiers.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant with a substantial Europe base, top countries: United States (~55%), United Kingdom (~10%), Canada (~7%), Germany (~5%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
