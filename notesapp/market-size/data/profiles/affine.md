# AFFiNE

## Overview
- VC-backed open-source startup; legal entity Toeverything Pte. Ltd., based in Singapore.
- Founded in 2020 by Jiachen He (CEO), Yifeng Wang, Yinan Long, and Chi Zhang; GitHub project publicly debuted August 2022.
- Employee count estimate: small-to-mid team, roughly 20-40 (estimated based on $18M raised across two seed rounds and typical open-core team sizing).

## Business Model
- Open-core: self-hosted/local-first product is free and open-source; monetization via hosted cloud sync, Pro, and Enterprise subscription tiers.
- Pricing: free tier plus paid Pro and Team/Enterprise plans (affine.pro/pricing lists tiers starting from $0, with Pro and Team pricing not consistently disclosed across secondary sources — treat exact figures as needing direct verification on affine.pro).
- Target customer: privacy-focused individuals, self-hosters, and teams wanting a combined docs + whiteboard + database workspace as a Notion/Miro alternative with full data control.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: unifies document editing, infinite-canvas whiteboarding, and structured databases in one local-first app — explicitly pitched as "there can be more than Notion and Miro," i.e., merging two categories competitors keep separate.
- Platform availability: Web, desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux), mobile, and self-hosted via Docker; local-first architecture with optional cloud sync.
- Key features: block-based editor, infinite canvas/whiteboard mode, bi-directional linking, AI-assisted writing/summarization, end-to-end data portability and offline-first storage.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed; no reliable third-party ARR estimate found.
- Adoption proxy: reached 10,000 GitHub stars within 43 days of its August 2022 launch and has grown to 70,000+ GitHub stars as of mid-2026, a strong open-source adoption signal among self-hosters and privacy-minded teams.
- Funding history: $18M raised total across two seed rounds (investors not fully itemized in available sources).
- Growth trajectory: rapid early GitHub star growth and continued accumulation to 70K+ stars indicates sustained developer/community interest, though paying-customer conversion is not publicly quantified.

## Competitive Position
AFFiNE sits in the same crowded "open-source all-in-one workspace" niche as AppFlowy, Notion, and Anytype, differentiating primarily through its combined docs-plus-whiteboard product surface and stronger early funding base ($18M vs. AppFlowy's $6.4M). Its moat is moderate: open-source trust and a genuinely differentiated canvas+doc hybrid UI, but it competes for the same privacy-conscious, self-hosting audience as several well-funded rivals, and category-defining incumbents (Notion, Miro) could add similar features at any time.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Open-core cloud upsell — Pro (~$5.99/month) and Believer (~$8/month) hosted subscription tiers on top of a free, self-hostable core.
- **Secondary monetization:** Team/self-hosted Team license (~$10/seat/month) and Enterprise/Installable License for larger self-hosted deployments requiring 10+ seats; no ads.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — rapid GitHub star growth (10K in 43 days, 70K+ by 2026) drives adoption, supplemented by a better-funded ($18M) team able to invest in some paid growth and enterprise sales-assist.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $60-150 (estimated) — open-core with a larger VC raise than most peers, implying more paid-growth capacity than a bootstrapped OSS project, though community/GitHub-led adoption still substitutes for much of the funnel.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $120-220 (estimated) — based on ~$5.99-8/month at an assumed ~24 month average paid retention.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~1.5-2:1 (estimated) — weaker than peers, consistent with its heavier funding load and unproven cloud-conversion economics noted in its Competitive Position.
- **User demographics:** Privacy-focused knowledge workers and developers wanting a combined docs+whiteboard tool, roughly 25-40, global with a skew toward Europe/Asia given its Singapore base.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global/distributed, developer-community-skewed, top countries: United States (~28%), Germany (~13%), India (~10%), China (~9%) — estimated from HQ location / open-source contributor base, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
