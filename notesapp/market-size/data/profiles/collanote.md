# CollaNote

## Overview
- Parent company: Zauberberg Lab Company Limited — small, privately held/independent developer; no institutional VC funding identified.
- Founded: Reported origin story traces to a student project at Hamburg University; specific incorporation year not confirmed by a primary source in this research pass.
- HQ: Not definitively confirmed in public sources reviewed (developer entity name suggests German/European origin, but current registered HQ location is unclear).
- Employees: Estimated very small team (likely under 10), consistent with an indie app of this scale — no official headcount disclosed.

## Business Model
- Freemium with in-app purchases; free app download with a paid "Pro" unlock.
- Pricing: Reported figures include roughly $5.95 (existing-user/upgrade pricing), $8.29 (launch pricing), and $11.90 (normal/list pricing) for the Pro unlock, per a third-party review (June 2026) — pricing appears to be a one-time or promotional-tier in-app purchase rather than a recurring subscription, though exact current structure should be verified directly on the App Store given limited primary-source detail.
- Target customer: Students and note-takers wanting real-time collaborative note-taking — its distinguishing target segment is groups/classes needing simultaneous multi-user editing, more so than solo note-takers.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: real-time collaboration with unlimited participants — allowing multiple users to write/annotate on the same notebook simultaneously, a feature not emphasized by Goodnotes/Notability.
- Platforms: iOS/iPadOS primarily (App Store listing confirmed); broader platform coverage (Android/Windows/Mac) not confirmed in this research pass.
- Key features: handwritten notes, PDF markup, digital planners, flashcards, and multi-user real-time collaboration combined into one app.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: Not publicly disclosed; given its small-indie scale and modest one-time-purchase pricing (under $12), revenue is estimated to be modest (likely low six figures to low seven figures annually at most) — this is a rough estimate based on app scale and pricing, not a sourced figure.
- Users: No official user or download counts found in public sources reviewed.
- Funding: No VC funding identified — appears to be a self-funded/bootstrapped indie project.
- Growth trajectory: A "CollaNote 2.0" relaunch/update has been covered by niche paperless/note-taking review outlets, suggesting ongoing but modest, community-driven development rather than aggressive growth investment.

## Competitive Position
CollaNote occupies a narrow niche as one of the few note-taking apps built specifically around real-time multi-user collaboration, which is a genuine feature differentiator versus the largely single-user-focused Goodnotes/Notability/Noteshelf. However, its small team, thin public financial/company footprint, and narrower platform coverage make it a minor player overall — its moat is the collaboration feature itself, but that is replicable by larger, better-funded competitors if they choose to prioritize it.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** One-time in-app purchase ("Pro" unlock, roughly $5.95–$11.90 depending on pricing tier/timing).
- **Secondary monetization:** None identified — no subscription, ads, enterprise tier, or data licensing found in public sources.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — free app download with paid Pro unlock, discovery via App Store search/browse and niche paperless/note-taking review outlets (e.g., beingpaperless.com coverage of the 2.0 relaunch); no evidence of paid marketing given the very small (likely <10-person) team.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $5–$25 (estimated). Low end of the indie freemium band, consistent with a small self-funded team relying entirely on organic App Store discovery and niche-community reviews.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $10–$30 (estimated), based on a single ~$6–$12 one-time Pro purchase with limited repeat-purchase potential (no recurring revenue mechanism identified) — structurally low relative to subscription competitors.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~1.3:1 (estimated). Thin and below the 3:1 healthy threshold, though plausible for a small indie project sustained by a niche loyal collaboration-focused user base rather than aggressive growth economics.
- **User demographics:** Students and study groups/classes seeking real-time collaborative note-taking, primarily on iPad; niche and narrower than mainstream single-user note apps — inferred from positioning and platform focus, as no official user data is disclosed.
- **Estimated user geography:** Asia-Pacific-skewing, top countries: Taiwan (~20%), Japan (~15%), South Korea (~12%) — estimated from platform reach (iPad-first collaborative note-taking, strong adoption in East Asian student/study-group markets) / HQ location (unconfirmed, possible European developer entity), not disclosed.
