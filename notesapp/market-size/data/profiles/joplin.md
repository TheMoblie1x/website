# Joplin

## Overview
- Open-source project, community/donor-funded; not a VC-backed company. Legally minimal commercial structure around the project.
- Created by Laurent Cozic, a French software engineer; project headquartered (nominally) in Nancy, France. Initial release circa 2017-2018.
- Employee count estimate: effectively 1 core paid/lead maintainer (Laurent Cozic), supported by 250+ volunteer open-source contributors on GitHub.

## Business Model
- Core app is entirely free and open source (freemium at the edges): the desktop/mobile note-taking app itself has no paywall.
- Revenue comes from: (1) an optional cloud sync subscription, Joplin Cloud, priced roughly $5–$13 CAD/month depending on tier, and (2) donations/sponsorships via Patreon, GitHub Sponsors, and other patron platforms.
- Target customer: privacy- and open-source-minded individual users (developers, technical/PKM enthusiasts) who want free note-taking with optional paid cloud sync.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: fully open-source, privacy-focused note-taking with strong end-to-end encryption support and flexible self-hosted or third-party sync (Nextcloud, WebDAV, Dropbox, OneDrive, S3, or Joplin Cloud).
- Platform availability: Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, plus a terminal (CLI) client and web clipper browser extensions.
- Key features: Markdown-based notes and to-dos, end-to-end encryption, multiple sync backend choices (no lock-in to one vendor), plugin/extension API, and a large, active open-source contributor community (52,000+ GitHub stars).

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not disclosed; primarily small-scale (patron donations + modest cloud subscription revenue) — likely low single-digit millions USD or less, estimated based on the scale of the project and lack of institutional funding.
- Funding: no venture funding; funded via Patreon/GitHub Sponsors and other patron contributions, plus Joplin Cloud subscription income.
- Adoption: approximately 11 million cumulative desktop downloads recorded by late 2025 (~7.3M Windows, ~2.1M macOS, ~1.6M Linux, per third-party estimate aggregation); 52,000+ GitHub stars.
- Growth trajectory: steady, community-driven growth; no disclosed revenue growth figures.

## Competitive Position
Joplin occupies the free/open-source, privacy-and-portability-first corner of the note-taking market, competing most directly with Standard Notes and Notesnook for privacy-conscious users, and indirectly with Obsidian for the plain-text/Markdown PKM audience. Its moat is trust from full transparency (open-source code, auditable encryption, no vendor lock-in on sync), but its minimal monetization and single-maintainer core team make it structurally fragile compared to funded competitors.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Donations/sponsorships (Patreon, GitHub Sponsors) plus optional Joplin Cloud sync subscription.
- **Secondary monetization:** None beyond the cloud subscription and donations — no ads, no enterprise tier, no data licensing.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Donation-funded / Organic — GitHub visibility (52,000+ stars), open-source contributor network, and word of mouth in privacy/dev communities drive adoption; no paid marketing.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $0–$10 (estimated). Essentially the floor of the indie-freemium-organic archetype — a single-maintainer, volunteer-supported OSS project with no marketing budget.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $120–$250 (estimated) — e.g., Joplin Cloud at ~$8 CAD/mo (~$6 USD) × ~24-month avg retention ≈ $150 for the small paying subset; most users never convert from the free app, and donation revenue per donor is typically small and irregular.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~20:1 (estimated) — very healthy per paying/donating user, but absolute revenue is small since conversion to any paid relationship is low.
- **User demographics:** Inferred from positioning: privacy- and open-source-minded individual users, disproportionately developers and technically literate PKM users, roughly 25–45, global with a Europe/tech-hub skew (project based in France); no official demographic data disclosed.

- **Estimated user geography:** Europe-heavy/Distributed, top countries: France (~15%), Germany (~13%), United States (~15%), United Kingdom (~8%), India (~7%) — estimated from HQ location (Nancy, France) and OSS/privacy-community concentration in Europe, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
