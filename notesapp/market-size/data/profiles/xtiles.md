# xTiles

## Overview
- Private, small startup; reported as largely unfunded/bootstrapped with some non-dilutive/accelerator-style support rather than traditional venture rounds.
- Founded circa 2021; based in Ukraine (team) with a US (Wilmington, Delaware) corporate registration typical of startups incorporating in the US for business purposes. Founders/co-founders reported across sources include Andrii Kusyi, Ksenia Iliuk, Maksym (Max) Kuchur, Andrey Tabunshchik, and Bogdan Romaniuk — sourcing is inconsistent across outlets, so exact founder attribution should be treated as approximate.
- Employee count: approximately 30 people as of 2025 (source: GetLatka).

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription SaaS visual workspace/notebook tool.
- Pricing (SaaSworthy/SoftwareSuggest listings): Plus plan from $8.25/user/month, Family plan around $12.42/month, Team plan around $24.92/month; a free plan with limited features is also offered. (Note: some aggregator listings show conflicting figures — treat exact numbers as approximate and verify on xtiles.app for current pricing.)
- Target customer: individuals, families, and small teams organizing projects and ideas visually — positioned between a notes app and a lightweight visual project-planning tool.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: a tile/card-based visual workspace ("digital notebook") that blends moodboard-style visual organization with structured note-taking and project planning, aimed at users who think visually rather than in linear documents or outlines.
- Platform availability: Web app, with desktop and mobile access noted in marketing materials; primarily browser-based.
- Key features: drag-and-drop tile/card layouts, embeds (links, images, files) within tiles, template library for common use cases (planning, brainstorming, goal tracking), and multi-tier team/family sharing plans.
- xTiles has represented Ukraine at international startup events (e.g., TechCrunch Disrupt), reflecting its identity as a Ukrainian-founded startup building for a global audience despite regional headwinds.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: reported at $3.3M in 2025 with a 30-person team (source: GetLatka).
- Users/customers: not officially disclosed in this research.
- Funding history: sources conflict — Crunchbase/Tracxn-style aggregation describes xTiles as largely unfunded by traditional VC, while other reporting notes support from programs such as UK-Ukraine TechBridge and Google for Startups (non-dilutive or program-based support rather than priced equity rounds). No specific dollar amount of institutional VC funding was confirmed.
- Growth trajectory: the jump to $3.3M reported revenue with a 30-person team in 2025 suggests meaningful growth from its 2021-2022 launch, consistent with a bootstrapped-to-profitable trajectory rather than one fueled by large VC rounds.

## Competitive Position
- xTiles competes in the visual workspace/notebook category alongside Milanote, Notion's gallery/board views, and to a lesser extent Heptabase's whiteboard approach, differentiated by its tile-first, moodboard-like default layout.
- Its moat is modest — the visual-tile UX is appealing and well-templated for specific use cases (planning, brainstorming) but is a feature that larger platforms (Notion, Miro) can and do replicate.
- Reported revenue growth to $3.3M with a lean team suggests a capital-efficient, product-led growth story; its long-term position depends on maintaining differentiation against increasingly visual-first features in larger incumbent tools.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user/per-plan subscription (Plus ~$8.25/user/month, Family ~$12.42/month, Team ~$24.92/month, on top of a limited free plan).
- **Secondary monetization:** Tiered upsell across Plus/Family/Team plans and a template library that supports conversion, but no distinct enterprise contracts, ads, or data licensing disclosed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — growth driven by content/SEO around visual planning and templates, community and accelerator-program exposure (Google for Startups, UK-Ukraine TechBridge, TechCrunch Disrupt appearances), and free-tier trial conversion rather than heavy paid-ads spend.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $15-$45 (estimated). A freemium consumer/prosumer app with some SMB self-serve team plans, blending indie-organic acquisition with modest content/program-driven marketing given its reported bootstrapped-to-profitable trajectory.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $200-$450 (estimated). Based on a blended ~$8-25/month price point across individual, family, and team plans and an estimated 18-24 month average retention.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~8:1 (estimated) — healthy (>3:1), consistent with the capital-efficient, product-led growth story described in the Financial Profile section.
- **User demographics:** Individuals, families, and small teams organizing projects and ideas visually, roughly age 22-40; inferred from positioning and tiered pricing (individual/family/team plans) rather than disclosed demographic data. Likely some skew toward Europe/Ukraine given the founding team's roots, alongside a broader global user base.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global/distributed with a notable Eastern European base, top countries: United States (~30%), Ukraine (~15%), Poland (~8%), United Kingdom (~7%) — estimated from Ukraine-based founding team/HQ alongside broader global platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
