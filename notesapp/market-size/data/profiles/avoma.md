# Avoma

## Overview
- Independent, privately held, VC-backed startup.
- Founded 2017 by Aditya Kothadiya (CEO), Devendra Laulkar, and Albert Lai; founded/headquartered in Palo Alto, California.
- Employee count estimate: roughly 80-120 (estimated based on $15M total funding raised, ~1,000+ customer base, and typical Series A-stage SaaS company sizing; not disclosed in sources).

## Business Model
- Subscription SaaS with modular add-ons: base "AI Meeting Assistant" plans plus paid Conversation Intelligence and Revenue Intelligence add-ons (unbundled as of 2026, previously bundled).
- Pricing (per user/month, annual billing): Startup $19, Organization $29, Enterprise $39. Conversation and Revenue Intelligence are now separate paid add-ons — a rep needing both on the Organization plan pays roughly $82/user/month all-in. Source: claap.io/blog/avoma-pricing, avoma.com/pricing.
- Target customer: revenue teams (sales, customer success) at mid-market and enterprise companies across software, financial services, healthcare, and professional services.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiators: an integrated four-in-one suite — AI Meeting Assistant (recording/transcription/notes), Scheduler & Lead Router, Conversation Intelligence (coaching), and Revenue Intelligence (deal/forecast tracking) — positioned as a lower-cost Gong/Chorus alternative.
- Platform availability: integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and major CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot).
- Key features: coaching scorecards, deal risk/forecast analytics, and scheduling/lead-routing bundled together, aiming to replace multiple point tools (a notetaker plus a separate conversation-intelligence platform) with one subscription.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not disclosed; estimated in the $10M-$25M ARR range (estimated, based on 1,000+ organizations at blended ACVs implied by per-seat pricing).
- Customers: over 1,000 organizations reported as of 2026 (avoma.com, claap.io).
- Funding: $15M total — $3M Seed led by K9 Ventures, and a $12M Series A led by Headline, with participation from HubSpot Ventures and the Zoom Apps Fund. Source: claap.io/blog/avoma-pricing, tracxn.com.
- Growth trajectory: steady, capital-efficient growth (only $15M raised since 2017) rather than the blitzscaling seen at Granola or Read AI; recent unbundling of Conversation/Revenue Intelligence as paid add-ons suggests a push to increase average revenue per account.

## Competitive Position
- Avoma occupies a value niche below Gong/Chorus in price but with a broader out-of-the-box feature set (scheduling + notetaking + coaching + forecasting) than single-purpose notetakers like tl;dv or Grain.
- Its moat is modest: it lacks Gong's brand dominance in enterprise revenue intelligence and lacks the viral consumer-grade UX of Granola or Read AI, leaving it positioned as a pragmatic, budget-conscious choice for SMB/mid-market revenue teams.
- Backing from strategic investors (HubSpot Ventures, Zoom Apps Fund) gives it distribution advantages through partner ecosystems, but its capital base is thin relative to newer, better-funded entrants.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Startup/Organization/Enterprise tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** Unbundled Conversation Intelligence and Revenue Intelligence paid add-ons (as of 2026), which materially raise ARPU for revenue-team accounts; no ads.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — trial-led signup funnel supplemented by direct sales outreach to mid-market/enterprise revenue teams, aided by strategic distribution via HubSpot Ventures and the Zoom Apps Fund.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $150–$400 (estimated) — higher than pure consumer notetakers given its sales-motion-heavy, revenue-team-focused go-to-market and add-on upsell process requiring more sales touch.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $1,000–$2,500 (estimated) — based on a blended ~$30-80/user/month all-in ARPU (with add-ons) and 24-36 month average retention typical of sticky revenue-ops tooling.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** roughly 5:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Sales and customer-success teams at mid-market and enterprise companies (software, financial services, healthcare, professional services); primarily US-based; 1,000+ organizations.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant, top countries: United States (~68%), Canada (~9%), United Kingdom (~7%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
