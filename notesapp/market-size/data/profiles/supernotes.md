# Supernotes

## Overview
- Private, small independent/angel-backed startup (originally UK-based, London).
- Founded circa 2020; HQ London, UK. Founders: Connor Neblett and Tobias Whetton.
- Employee count: very small team, estimated under 10 (consistent with its modest funding and niche scale — no official figure disclosed).

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription SaaS built around atomic, card-based notes.
- Pricing (supernotes.app/pricing): Starter/free plan with up to 100 cards and core mobile/desktop/web apps, fast sync, offline access, and collaboration; Unlimited plan at £6/month (£72/year) adding unlimited cards, AI features, and full API access. Student discounts available (50% off annual, up to 63% off a 4-year plan).
- Target customer: individuals and small collaborative groups who favor an atomic "card" note-taking philosophy — positioned partly as a social/shareable note-taking tool, appealing to students and knowledge-management enthusiasts.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: "atomic" card-based notes (each note is a small, taggable, linkable card) combined with a social/sharing angle — Supernotes originally launched with an emphasis on shareable, collaborative note "decks."
- Platform availability: Web, desktop apps, and mobile apps (iOS/Android), with fast sync and offline access.
- Key features: card-based atomic notes, tagging and bidirectional linking, real-time collaboration, and (in paid tier) AI features and API access for integrations/automation.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed; given its small team and modest £6/month pricing at presumably a niche user base, revenue is estimated to be in the low hundreds-of-thousands-of-dollars range annually (estimated based on team size and pricing, not disclosed).
- Users/customers: not publicly disclosed.
- Funding history: raised approximately $1.83M total (per Crunchbase aggregation), including a £150K pre-seed round (around February 2021) from Flamefinch Partners, with additional support from accelerator/program investors including King's20 Accelerator, the NatWest Entrepreneur Accelerator, and UCL EDUCATE.
- Growth trajectory: no major funding rounds reported since its 2021 pre-seed, suggesting Supernotes has operated as a small, likely close-to-bootstrapped business since its early accelerator-backed start rather than pursuing venture-scale growth.

## Competitive Position
- Supernotes occupies a small niche within the card/atomic-note style of PKM tools, competing loosely with Notion's database views, RemNote's card-like blocks, and other outliner/card hybrids, but at a much smaller scale than any of them.
- Its moat is limited — the atomic-card UX and light collaboration/sharing angle are appealing but not structurally defensible against larger, better-funded competitors that can replicate card-style notes as a feature.
- Likely to persist as a small, loyal-niche lifestyle business rather than scale meaningfully; its low pricing and modest funding suggest limited resources for aggressive growth or AI feature investment relative to peers like RemNote or Heptabase.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (Unlimited plan, £6/month or £72/year, on top of a free Starter tier).
- **Secondary monetization:** Bundled AI features and API access within the paid tier; student discounts (up to 63% off multi-year plans) function as an acquisition lever rather than a distinct revenue stream. No enterprise tier, ads, or data licensing disclosed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — small-team, low-marketing-spend growth via word of mouth, the product's social/shareable-deck angle, and student-community discounts, consistent with its modest ~$1.83M total funding and lean team.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $5-$25 (estimated). A small, largely bootstrapped indie freemium app with minimal marketing budget, relying almost entirely on organic and community-driven discovery.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $100-$250 (estimated). Based on the ~$7.50/month-equivalent price and an estimated 18-24 month retention typical of a niche, loyal-but-modest-scale user base.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~7:1 (estimated) — healthy (>3:1), plausible for a low-overhead, community-driven niche product, though absolute revenue scale remains small.
- **User demographics:** Individuals and small collaborative groups favoring atomic/card-based notes, including students (via discount targeting) and knowledge-management enthusiasts, roughly age 20-35; inferred from positioning and pricing rather than disclosed data. Likely skews UK/Europe given London origins and GBP pricing.
- **Estimated user geography:** Europe, top countries: United Kingdom (~30%), United States (~25%), Germany (~10%), Netherlands (~7%) — estimated from London HQ and GBP-denominated pricing, not disclosed.
