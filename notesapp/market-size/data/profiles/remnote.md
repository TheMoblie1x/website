# RemNote

## Overview
- Private, small VC-backed startup (RemNote Inc.).
- Founded 2020; HQ San Francisco (originally started at TU Delft/Netherlands roots before relocating). Founders: Moritz Wallawitsch and Martin Schneider.
- Employee count: approximately 18-19 employees as of 2025-2026 (source: GetLatka/Wellfound aggregated estimates).

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription SaaS combining note-taking with spaced-repetition flashcards and AI study tools.
- Pricing (remnote.com/pricing, current): Free plan ($0, unlimited notes/flashcards, 3 annotated PDFs, 250 AI credits); Pro plan $8/month ($96 billed yearly) with PDF annotation, image occlusion, and 1,000 monthly AI credits; Pro with AI plan $18/month ($216 billed yearly) with 20,000 monthly AI credits, lecture recording, and AI grading.
- Target customer: students (especially university/med/law students using spaced repetition for exam prep) and self-directed learners; less oriented toward general knowledge workers than competitors like Notion or Obsidian.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: tight integration of outliner-style note-taking with built-in spaced-repetition flashcards generated directly from notes, plus PDF annotation and AI-assisted study tools — a study-workflow-first take on PKM.
- Platform availability: Web, desktop apps (macOS/Windows/Linux via Electron), and mobile apps (iOS/Android).
- Key features: automatic flashcard generation from notes, hierarchical outliner with bidirectional linking, PDF/EPUB annotation with active recall prompts, and AI credits for summarization/grading/generation tasks.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: estimated around $2M in annual recurring revenue (source: GetLatka).
- Users/customers: not officially disclosed; RemNote has marketed itself to a large student user base globally but no official total user count was found in this research.
- Funding history: raised a total of approximately $2.8M, with its most recent institutional round closing around September 2021; GetLatka reports a $5.9M valuation tied to a 2025 "M&A Offer" round, suggesting a small/strategic transaction rather than a traditional growth-stage VC round.
- Growth trajectory: RemNote appears to be a modestly-funded, steady-growth company relative to peers — its funding-to-revenue ratio (~$2.8M raised against ~$2M estimated ARR) suggests capital-efficient, close-to-sustainable operations rather than hypergrowth burn.

## Competitive Position
- RemNote's niche is the intersection of note-taking and spaced-repetition learning science, putting it in a category largely distinct from general PKM tools (Notion, Obsidian) and closer to Anki plus a notes app.
- Its moat comes from the tightness of the notes-to-flashcard workflow integration, which is harder to replicate quickly than generic notes features, but Anki's free/open-source dominance in pure spaced repetition and Notion's AI expansion both pressure RemNote from adjacent angles.
- Best positioned as a durable, profitable-leaning niche player serving serious students rather than a broad horizontal PKM platform; unlikely to achieve venture-scale outcomes without meaningfully expanding beyond the student use case.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (Pro $8/month, Pro with AI $18/month, on top of a free tier).
- **Secondary monetization:** AI-credit-based upsell (higher monthly AI credit allotments gate the top tier); no enterprise contracts, ads, or data licensing disclosed. Some potential for institutional/education channel deals given its student focus, though none confirmed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — driven by student word-of-mouth, study-technique content/SEO (spaced repetition, study-with-me communities), and free-tier trial conversion rather than paid advertising, consistent with its capital-efficient funding profile.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $10-$35 (estimated). Indie freemium consumer app targeting price-sensitive students via organic/App Store discovery and content marketing, at the lower end given the modest ~$2.8M total funding raised.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $150-$350 (estimated). Based on a blended $8-18/month price point and an estimated 18-24 month retention window tied to a student's active study/exam-prep period before natural churn (graduation, course completion).
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~8:1 (estimated) — healthy (>3:1), consistent with the capital-efficient, close-to-sustainable operating profile described in the Financial Profile section.
- **User demographics:** University, medical, and law students and self-directed learners using spaced repetition for exam prep, roughly age 18-28; inferred from product positioning (flashcards, PDF annotation for coursework) rather than disclosed demographic data. Likely a global student base given the product's remote/Delft academic origins.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global/distributed student base skewing North America/Europe, top countries: United States (~35%), Germany/Netherlands (~15%), United Kingdom (~10%), India (~8%) — estimated from SF HQ, Delft/Netherlands academic roots, and global student-user positioning, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
