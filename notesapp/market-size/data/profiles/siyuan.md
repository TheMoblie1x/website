# SiYuan

## Overview
- Small independent/commercial open-source project (not VC-backed); operated by a small China-based team/company behind the siyuan-note GitHub organization. No evidence of institutional funding.
- Founding year not precisely confirmed in available sources; project has been active for several years with steady releases (version history through 3.9.x as of 2026). No public HQ or named founders beyond the GitHub org "siyuan-note."
- Employee count estimate: very small, likely fewer than 10 (estimated based on a single-repo, self-funded commercial open-source model typical of solo/small-team dev shops).

## Business Model
- Open-source core (AGPL-3.0) is completely free for local/offline use, including AI integration, databases, and bidirectional links; monetization is limited to optional paid add-ons for official cloud sync and storage.
- Pricing (per openalternative.co, 2026): "PRO" tier ~$64 one-time and a "Subscription"/lifetime tier ~$148 one-time, covering official cloud sync, third-party sync (S3/WebDAV), and a cloud inbox — core app functionality remains free regardless.
- Target customer: privacy-first individual knowledge workers and self-hosters who want a fully-featured Notion/Obsidian/OneNote alternative without recurring subscription costs.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: a block-level editor with bidirectional links, native plugin system, and direct LLM integration, built on plain Markdown files with Git-based version control — emphasizing full local data ownership plus generous free-tier feature parity with paid competitors.
- Platform availability: Desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux), mobile, and self-hosted via Docker; can run as a local server accessible over LAN from a phone, enabling small-team or personal multi-device use without a cloud subscription.
- Key features: block-based Markdown editor, bidirectional linking/backlinks, plugin ecosystem, end-to-end encrypted incremental sync (paid), Git-based version control, optional AI agent collaboration features.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: not disclosed; likely small, driven by one-time PRO/lifetime sync purchases rather than recurring SaaS revenue (estimated based on its one-time-purchase pricing model rather than subscription).
- Adoption proxy: approximately 46,300 GitHub stars as of September 2026 (global rank ~#567 per star-history.com), indicating solid but smaller adoption than category leaders like Memos or AppFlowy.
- Funding history: no institutional/VC funding identified; appears to be a self-funded, revenue-sustained open-source commercial project.
- Growth trajectory: consistent release cadence (multiple point releases through 2026) suggests active, ongoing development; no public growth metrics beyond GitHub stars.

## Competitive Position
SiYuan competes in the crowded self-hosted, privacy-first note-taking space (alongside TriliumNext, Memos, Logseq, Obsidian) by offering unusually generous free functionality — including AI and databases — reserving payment only for cloud sync convenience. Its moat is weak in a commercial sense (no funding, small team, easily forkable AGPL codebase) but its one-time-purchase pricing and China-market fluency give it a loyal niche audience distinct from subscription-based Western competitors.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** One-time purchase — PRO (~$64 one-time) and Subscription/lifetime (~$148 one-time) tiers unlocking official cloud sync and storage, on top of a fully free core app.
- **Secondary monetization:** None beyond the sync add-on; no enterprise tier, no ads, no API/data licensing.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — a self-funded, small China-based team relying entirely on GitHub visibility, word of mouth in privacy/self-hosting communities, and its China-market presence rather than any paid acquisition.

- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $64-148 (estimated) — since the paid product is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, LTV is simply the purchase price (no recurring revenue to compound with retention assumptions).
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~8:1 (estimated), using a $12 midpoint CAC against a ~$100 midpoint one-time purchase.
