# Supernormal

## Overview
- Independent, privately held startup (previously briefly rebranded as "Radiant"; reverted to the Supernormal brand in February 2026).
- Founded year not confirmed in available sources (product has existed since at least early 2020s); headquarters not confirmed in available sources.
- Employee count estimate: small team, roughly 15-30 (estimated based on limited funding visibility and niche market position; not disclosed).

## Business Model
- Credit-based, freemium subscription SaaS.
- Pricing: Free tier (limited functionality); Pro $18/user/month; Business $32/user/month with team features; annual billing gives ~20% discount. Model is credit-based, so teams pay for the volume of AI credits/meetings used. Source: capterra.com/p/246530/Supernormal, summarizemeeting.com.
- Target customer: sales teams needing bot-free (no visible bot in participant list) meeting capture plus an AI agent that generates sales deliverables (briefs, follow-ups).

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiators: bot-free/botless capture that works quietly in the background across Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack Huddles, and even in-person meetings — avoiding the "notetaker bot joined the call" experience common to competitors.
- Platform availability: native desktop app for Mac and Windows.
- Key features: AI agent that turns meeting context directly into completed work products (campaign briefs, pitch decks, project proposals, follow-up emails, status updates), moving beyond passive note-taking into active deliverable generation.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not disclosed; no reliable estimate available given lack of funding/user data in public sources.
- Users/customers: not disclosed in available sources.
- Funding: no confirmed funding rounds found in available sources; Crunchbase profile exists but round details were not surfaced in search results. Treat as undisclosed/private, likely bootstrapped or lightly funded (estimated based on absence of press coverage typical of well-funded raises).
- Growth trajectory: the February 2026 rebrand from "Radiant" back to "Supernormal," paired with expansion into AI-agent-generated deliverables, signals active product investment despite low funding visibility, but the company has far less market presence than Granola, Grain, or tl;dv.

## Competitive Position
- Supernormal's main differentiator — invisible, bot-free capture — addresses a real pain point (meeting participants disliking visible recording bots), giving it a defensible niche for sales teams in trust-sensitive contexts.
- However, it has comparatively low public visibility, no confirmed major funding round, and its recent rebrand/pivot signals it is still searching for a durable market position.
- It lacks the moat needed to compete with better-funded, higher-profile competitors (Granola, Read AI, Gong) and risks being squeezed by free bundled offerings like Zoom AI Companion.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user SaaS subscription (Pro/Business tiers, credit-based usage).
- **Secondary monetization:** Credit-based overage/usage pricing on top of the base subscription; no ads; no confirmed enterprise add-on tier.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — freemium credit-based trial funnel targeting sales teams that value bot-free capture, with limited visible paid-marketing spend given low funding.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $60–$200 (estimated) — likely leans organic/word-of-mouth given minimal disclosed funding for paid acquisition, but sales-team targeting implies some outbound cost.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $250–$600 (estimated) — based on ~$18-32/user/month ARPU and a shorter estimated 12-18 month average retention given the company's thinner market presence and higher churn risk versus better-funded peers.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** roughly 3:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Sales teams and trust-sensitive knowledge workers preferring bot-free capture; small-to-mid-market; geography not confirmed in public sources.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-leaning with a Europe secondary base, top countries: United States (~55%), United Kingdom (~12%), Canada (~8%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
