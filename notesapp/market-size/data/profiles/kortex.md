# Kortex

## Overview
- Private, independent startup; publicly states a preference for community/self-funding over venture capital (no confirmed VC rounds found).
- Founding year and HQ not clearly disclosed in public sources; positions itself around a "founding thesis" focused on writers/creators (kortex.co/blog).
- Employee count: not publicly disclosed (estimated: small startup team, likely under 20, based on product scope and independent-funding posture).

## Business Model
- Freemium SaaS subscription model with tiered AI usage limits.
- Pricing (from kortex.co/pricing):
  - Free: $0/month — 15 Kortex AI requests/month (base models only), 5GB storage, 5MB file uploads, 5 voice transcriptions/month, 1 member/1 workspace.
  - Kore: $10/month (billed annually) or $14/month month-to-month — unlimited base-model AI requests, 15GB storage, 50MB uploads, 250 voice transcriptions/month, 3 members/3 workspaces.
  - Premium: $17.50/month (annual) or $21/month — unlimited base + 500 premium AI requests/month, 25GB storage, 1GB uploads, unlimited transcriptions, up to 100 members/10 workspaces, private community access.
  - Custom: contact sales, for large teams.
- Target customer: writers, creators, students, and marketers who want an AI-augmented personal knowledge/writing workspace (a "second brain" for creative workflows).

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: "kAI" assistant that summarizes, researches, and edits using the user's own documents, highlights, and external sources — tightly coupled to a writing/publishing workflow rather than generic note-taking.
- Platform availability: web app; mobile and offline mode referenced as newer/upcoming releases (per Kortex's January release blog).
- Key features: AI-assisted writing and research, highlight capture, Readwise integration, voice transcription, private community/strategy library for premium subscribers, creator-focused content tools.

## Financial Profile (estimated)
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed; no third-party estimate found. Given freemium pricing ($10–$21/user/month) and small scale, estimated low-single-digit-million ARR at most (estimated based on pricing tier and apparent team size).
- Adoption: user/customer counts not publicly disclosed.
- Funding: no confirmed institutional funding rounds found; company messaging explicitly favors community/bootstrapped funding over VC.
- Growth trajectory: evidence of active product iteration (AI model upgrades, new pricing tiers, Black Friday promotions) suggesting an actively growing but still early-stage business.

## Competitive Position
Kortex competes in the crowded AI-note-taking/second-brain category (alongside Notion AI, Mem, Reflect, Tana, and mymind) but narrows its focus to writers and content creators, pairing note capture with an AI writing/research assistant and a creator community. Its moat is thin relative to larger, better-funded competitors (Notion, Microsoft, Google) that can bundle similar AI features into existing platforms; Kortex's durability depends on niche community loyalty and product focus rather than technology lock-in or scale.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Subscription (freemium SaaS, tiered by AI usage/storage/members).
- **Secondary monetization:** Team/enterprise upsell (custom "contact sales" tier for large teams); private community access bundled into Premium as a soft add-on.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — free tier with capped AI requests drives trial usage, converting writers/creators into paid Kore/Premium tiers; niche creator-community positioning substitutes for large paid-acquisition spend.
- **Estimated CAC (estimated):** $30–$100. Small independent/bootstrapped team, likely organic/content + creator-community-driven acquisition, consistent with indie consumer subscription economics.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $120–$350 (estimated). Based on $10–$21/month tiers with an assumed 1.5–2.5 year average retention given a competitive, feature-overlapping category (Notion AI, Mem, Reflect, Tana) that raises churn risk versus mymind.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~3:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Individual writers, creators, students, and marketers seeking an AI-augmented writing/research workspace; likely skews younger (20s–30s) and content-creator-adjacent versus mymind's broader knowledge-worker base.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-led with meaningful Europe presence, top countries: United States (~50%), United Kingdom (~10%), Canada (~7%) — estimated from English-language creator/writer positioning and undisclosed HQ, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
