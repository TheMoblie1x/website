# Mintlify

## Overview
- Private company, VC-backed; no parent company.
- Founded circa 2022 (Y Combinator-backed); HQ in San Francisco, California.
- Employee count estimate: approximately 62 employees as of 2026 (source: company data aggregation).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription for developer documentation hosting and generation, with an AI-native, docs-as-code workflow.
- Pricing: Specific public tier pricing not captured in available sources, but the company markets to a large base of developer-tool companies; monetization is subscription-based tied to documentation hosting/seats/features (exact $ tiers not confirmed in this research pass — flagged as not independently verified).
- Target customer: Developer-tool and API-first companies wanting fast, AI-assisted, beautifully designed documentation sites without heavy engineering investment.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: AI-native documentation platform emphasizing fast setup, polished design out of the box, and AI-assisted content generation/maintenance (e.g., auto-generating docs from code, AI chat over docs content) — positioned as a modern, faster-growing alternative to GitBook and ReadMe.
- Platform availability: Web-based SaaS, docs-as-code (Git-based) workflow, OpenAPI integration for API reference generation.
- Key features: AI-powered documentation generation and Q&A, git-based content workflow, fast onboarding/setup, and design-forward default themes that require minimal customization effort.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: Reported to have hit $10M ARR at the end of 2025, up roughly 10x from $1M ARR at the end of 2024 — a well-documented hypergrowth trajectory (source: Mintlify blog/press coverage).
- Customers: Powers documentation for over 20,000 companies (company-reported figure as of the Series B announcement).
- Funding history: Raised an $18M Series A in September 2024 led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Bain Capital Ventures and Y Combinator. Raised a $45M Series B in April 2026 at a $500M valuation, led by a16z and Salesforce Ventures, with participation from Bain Capital Ventures, Y Combinator, DST Global's Rahul Mehta, MVP Ventures, Avra, HubSpot Ventures, and TwentyTwo Ventures (sources: Mintlify blog, FinSMEs).
- Growth trajectory: Extremely fast growth — 10x revenue growth in one year and a $500M valuation roughly 18 months after Series A — positions Mintlify as the fastest-growing company in this documentation-tools peer set.

## Competitive Position
Mintlify is the clear momentum leader among developer-documentation tools in this batch, combining AI-native product design with top-tier venture backing (a16z, Salesforce Ventures) and a $500M valuation on $10M ARR — a rich multiple reflecting investor confidence in continued hypergrowth. Its moat is still being built (docs tooling has historically had low switching costs), but its AI-first workflow and design quality currently give it a meaningful edge over incumbents like GitBook and ReadMe, which have grown more slowly and raised comparatively little capital.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Freemium SaaS with metered AI-credit consumption (free Starter tier, paid Pro tier ~$450–$540/mo, custom Enterprise)
- **Secondary monetization:** Usage-based AI credit packs ($100–$1,000+/month tiers, $0.01/credit overage) layered on top of subscription tiers; no ads
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic (dev-tool virality/content) — fully free core product turns developer-authored docs into a distribution/SEO engine, converting usage-heavy teams to paid AI credits and Enterprise contracts.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $300–$1,500 (estimated) — low-cost, product-led/content-driven acquisition typical of free-to-start dev tools; enterprise deals add modest inside-sales cost.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $2,500–$10,000 (estimated) — assumes ~$1,000–$3,000 average annual spend (Pro subscription plus AI credit overages) and a ~3-year customer lifetime; wide range reflects usage-based variability.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated)
- **User demographics:** Developer-tool and API-first startups through mid-market software companies; engineering and DevRel/docs teams are the primary users and buyers; global, with US tech-hub concentration reflected in its $500M valuation/venture backing.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global, US-weighted, top countries: United States (~45%), India (~10%), United Kingdom (~7%), Germany (~6%) — estimated from HQ location (San Francisco, CA) and dev-tool community reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
