# Fibery

## Overview
- Private, VC-backed but very lightly funded (near-bootstrapped in practice); no parent company.
- Founded in 2017 by Michael Dubakov and Vadim Gaidukevich; headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus.
- Employee count: approximately 32-36 people as of 2025-2026 (estimated, aggregator-sourced) — a small, focused team. Source: [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/fibery/__EO_ttECLdzntVVDlAtEH2gBKZsvBCqF4oAASEc01g7U).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription with tiered per-seat pricing: Free, Standard, Pro, and Enterprise levels, with discounts for non-profits and open-source projects.
- Specific current dollar figures for each tier were not reliably retrievable from available sources at time of research; historically Fibery has priced competitively against Notion/Airtable/Monday.com in the $10-20/user/month range for mid-tiers (not independently confirmed — treat exact figures as unverified and check fibery.io/pricing directly for current numbers).
- Target customer: product, research, and operations teams at startups and scale-ups who need a highly customizable, connected work-management system rather than a rigid, template-based tool.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: an unusually flexible, "process-agnostic" work-management platform where users build custom "apps" and "entities" with relational links between them, letting teams model product management, CRM, HR, research, and documentation workflows in one connected graph of data — positioned as deeper than Notion for product-development use cases and more flexible than Airtable for cross-functional process modeling.
- Platform availability: web app (cloud-based); no dedicated native desktop/mobile apps emphasized in marketing, reflecting its smaller scale relative to competitors.
- Key features: linked "entities" across apps for end-to-end traceability (e.g., feedback → feature → task), custom automations, whiteboards, docs, and AI features for summarizing interviews, analyzing sentiment, and surfacing relevant feedback ("Fibery 2.0" product-discovery push).

- Users/customers: not disclosed at a reliable, sourced scale; company markets itself as serving product/research teams rather than publishing customer counts.
- Funding history: raised approximately $8.3M in total funding over 2 rounds, including a $5.2M Series A led by Tal Ventures with participation from Altair Capital; other investors cited include ToDay Ventures. - Growth trajectory: small but apparently profitable-trending trajectory; the company has stayed lean and independent rather than chasing hypergrowth, similar in spirit (if not scale) to Basecamp.

## Competitive Position
Fibery competes in a crowded "flexible work OS" category against Notion, Airtable, ClickUp, and Monday.com, differentiating primarily through deep relational data modeling suited to product-development and research workflows rather than broad horizontal appeal. Its moat is thin — a small team and modest funding relative to well-capitalized competitors — but it has carved a loyal niche among product teams who find Notion too document-centric and Airtable too spreadsheet-centric for connected process work. Long-term survival depends on retaining this specialist niche rather than competing head-on for mass-market share.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Free/Standard/Pro/Enterprise tiers, roughly $10-20/user/month for mid-tiers, unconfirmed exact figures).
- **Secondary monetization:** AI features for summarizing interviews, sentiment analysis, and feedback surfacing (part of the "Fibery 2.0" product-discovery push), plus discounts for non-profits/open-source rather than upsell revenue.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — a small, lean team relying on content marketing (e.g., its "next wave of work management" blog thought-leadership) and word-of-mouth among product/research teams rather than paid acquisition or a sales org.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $150–$600 (estimated). As a near-bootstrapped, small-team company with no visible sales force, Fibery likely acquires customers primarily through low-cost organic/content channels, similar to Basecamp but with less brand scale, keeping CAC in the lower-to-mid self-serve PLG range.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $3,600–$6,000 (estimated), assuming a small team of ~4-8 seats at ~$15/seat/month (~$1,200-$2,000 ACV) retained roughly 3 years — a loyal niche customer base but modest scale given the company's very low disclosed/estimated revenue (~$0.4M-$1.8M ARR).
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated) — reasonably healthy, though the company's small scale and wide revenue-estimate discrepancy suggest low confidence in these figures.
- **User demographics:** Product, research, and operations teams at startups and scale-ups seeking deep relational/connected data modeling; a specialist niche audience rather than mass-market, with limited geographic-skew data available (company is Cyprus-headquartered).
- **Estimated user geography:** Mixed US-customer-base / Eastern-Europe-origin team, top countries: United States (~35%), Russia (~12%), Cyprus (~8%), Georgia (~7%), United Kingdom (~6%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
