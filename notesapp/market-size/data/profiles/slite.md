# Slite

## Overview
- Privately held, VC-backed; not part of a larger parent company.
- Founded in 2016, headquartered in San Francisco, CA (originally founded in Paris, France) by Christophe Pasquier (CEO) among others.
- Employee count estimate: ~37-39 employees (2024-2025 estimates from Crunchbase/Tracxn-derived sources).

## Business Model
- Per-seat SaaS subscription with a freemium entry tier, monetizing through Standard and Premium/Enterprise plans billed monthly or annually.
- Pricing (public plans, approximate): Free tier for small teams; Standard tier priced per member/month (low-to-mid single digits to ~$10 range); Premium/Enterprise tiers with custom pricing for larger orgs (exact current figures fluctuate; verify on slite.com/pricing before quoting to clients).
- Target customer: SMB and mid-market teams, particularly startups and remote-first companies seeking a lightweight, fast internal wiki/knowledge base with strong Slack/AI integrations.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: minimalist, fast, Notion-style editor purpose-built for internal knowledge management (as opposed to Notion's broader all-in-one workspace/database ambitions), with an emphasis on structured "Q&A" and AI-assisted answers (Slite AI / "Ask").
- Platform availability: web app, native macOS/Windows desktop apps, iOS/Android mobile apps, and deep Slack integration.
- Key features: channel-based knowledge organization, collaborative real-time docs, AI-powered search and auto-answering of team questions, templates, and integrations with Slack, Google Drive, and other workplace tools.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue not publicly disclosed; estimated in the low-to-mid single-digit millions ARR (estimated based on funding size, headcount of ~37 employees, and typical SaaS revenue-per-employee benchmarks for a company at this funding stage).
- Customer counts not publicly disclosed with precision; marketing materials cite thousands of teams using the product, concentrated in startup/SMB segments.
- Funding history: total raised ~$15.5M across two rounds — a ~$4.5M seed round (2018) and an $11M Series A (2020) led by Spark Capital, with participation from Index Ventures and Y Combinator (Slite was a Y Combinator alum). No disclosed valuation.
- Growth trajectory: no major new funding rounds reported since 2020; the company appears to be running lean/capital-efficient rather than pursuing aggressive VC-fueled growth.

## Competitive Position
Slite occupies a niche as a lightweight, opinionated alternative to Confluence and Notion for smaller, remote-first teams that want a "just works" internal wiki with less setup overhead. Its moat is thin — the core editor/wiki functionality is easily replicated by well-funded competitors (Notion, Confluence) and its AI features are catching up to, not ahead of, the market. Longevity likely depends on defensible positioning around simplicity and Slack-native workflows rather than platform breadth or enterprise scale.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Standard/Premium/Enterprise tiers) with a freemium entry point.
- **Secondary monetization:** Tier upgrades that gate AI features (Slite AI/"Ask") and admin controls to Premium/Enterprise; no ads, no marketplace, no professional-services line.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — freemium signup drives the bulk of volume, with light-touch sales for larger accounts expanding from an existing free team.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $300–$1,200 (estimated) — a low-ACV, self-serve SMB PLG tool with a lean ~37-person team and no evidence of a heavy paid-acquisition or outbound sales engine.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $2,000–$6,000 (estimated) — assuming a typical paying team of 10–30 seats at ~$6–$10/seat/month (roughly $1,000–$2,500 ACV) and a 3–4 year average customer lifetime, shorter than enterprise peers given a startup/SMB-heavy base with higher churn risk.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~4:1 (estimated) — reasonably healthy for a lean, capital-efficient PLG company that hasn't raised since 2020.
- **User demographics:** SMB and startups, roughly 10–200 employees, remote-first tech companies and agencies; some larger outlier logos (Airbnb, Spotify, WeTransfer) cited in marketing; US/Europe geography given Paris origins and current SF HQ.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America and Europe, top countries: United States (~40%), France (~18%), United Kingdom (~10%), Germany (~8%) — estimated from Paris founding origins, current SF HQ, and remote-first tech customer base, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
