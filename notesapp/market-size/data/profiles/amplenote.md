# Amplenote

## Overview
- Parent company: Amplenote (independent, privately held); appears self-funded/bootstrapped with no major VC rounds identified in this research.
- Founded: Publicly available founding-year detail is limited; the product has been actively developed and marketed since the late 2010s/early 2020s (exact founding year not confirmed by a primary source in this pass — treat as unconfirmed).
- HQ: Not definitively confirmed in public sources reviewed; operates as a remote/distributed small team typical of the category.
- Employees: Estimated very small team (likely under 15), consistent with an indie productivity SaaS of its scale — no official headcount disclosed.

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription model with a 14-day free trial on paid tiers.
- Pricing (per amplenote.com/help and third-party aggregators, 2026): Personal (Free) — unlimited devices, all four modes (Jots, Notes, Tasks, Calendar), 5MB file-upload cap, no calendar sync; Pro — from about $5.84/month, adds native desktop app, Graph View, external calendar sync, larger file uploads; Unlimited — $10/month, adds encrypted notes and public publishing; Founder — $20/month, early-adopter tier with product-influence perks and priority support.
- Target customer: Productivity/PKM power users and professionals who want combined task management + note-taking + calendar in one tool — not primarily students or handwriting users, more of a text/typing-first knowledge-worker audience.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: unifies note-taking, task management, and calendar/daily planning in a single tool with a strong emphasis on actionable notes (turning notes into tasks) rather than static note storage.
- Platforms: Web app (primary), with native desktop apps (Pro tier and above) and mobile apps; no dedicated stylus/handwriting input — it is a typing/text-first tool, distinct from the handwriting-first apps in this comparison set.
- Key features: bidirectional note-task linking, Graph View for visualizing note connections, public note publishing, end-to-end encryption (on higher tiers), and integrated calendar.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: Not publicly disclosed; given indie-scale team and subscription pricing, revenue is estimated in the low single-digit millions of dollars annually at most — an estimate based on team size and market niche, not a sourced figure.
- Users: No official user counts found in public sources reviewed.
- Funding: No institutional VC funding rounds identified — appears to be a bootstrapped/founder-funded business.
- Growth trajectory: Consistent, incremental feature shipping (Graph View, encryption, publishing) suggests steady organic growth typical of a niche PKM SaaS rather than venture-scale expansion.

## Competitive Position
Amplenote sits in the "notes + tasks + calendar" PKM category alongside tools like Notion, Craft, and Sunsama rather than directly competing with handwriting-first apps — it is a marginal/adjacent player in the specific "handwriting/student note-taking" space defined for this research batch, since it has no stylus/handwriting focus. Its moat is the tight task-note-calendar integration and transparent, founder-accessible development model, but it lacks the brand scale, funding, or mobile/tablet handwriting UX of Goodnotes or Notability.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (Pro/Unlimited/Founder tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** Tiered feature upsells (native desktop app, Graph View, calendar sync, encrypted notes, public publishing), plus a "Founder" premium tier that monetizes early-adopter/product-influence access; no ads or data licensing identified.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — free Personal tier funneling into paid tiers via App Store/web discovery, PKM community word-of-mouth (Reddit, productivity forums), and content/SEO; small team size implies minimal paid-ads spend.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $10–$40 (estimated). Indie freemium/organic band consistent with a small, bootstrapped team relying on community and content-driven discovery rather than paid UA.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $100–$300 (estimated), based on blended Pro/Unlimited pricing (~$5.84–$10/month, i.e. ~$70–$120/year) retained for roughly 2–3 years among engaged PKM power users who deeply integrate the tool into daily workflows.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~8:1 (estimated). Very healthy, well above the 3:1 bar, typical of a sticky niche productivity subscription with low-cost organic acquisition.
- **User demographics:** Productivity/PKM power users and knowledge-work professionals combining notes, tasks, and calendar, roughly 25–45; not students or handwriting users. Geography skew inferred as US/global-English-speaking web-first audience, not disclosed directly.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global/Distributed, top countries: United States (~40%), United Kingdom (~10%), Canada (~8%) — estimated from HQ location (remote/distributed team, no confirmed HQ) / platform reach (English-language web-first PKM positioning), not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
