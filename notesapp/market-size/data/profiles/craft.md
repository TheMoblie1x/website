# Craft

## Overview
- Private, VC-backed company (Craft Docs Limited / "Craft Docs Limited, Inc.").
- Founded 2019 by Balint Orosz; based in Budapest, Hungary (with international operations). Product (Craft) launched on macOS/iOS in 2020, winning an Apple Design Award / Mac App of the Year recognition.
- Employee count estimate: mid-size startup scale, consistent with a Series B company (headcount not precisely disclosed; likely in the tens to low hundreds).

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription SaaS: free tier for individuals with core note/document features, paid tiers for advanced collaboration, storage, and AI features.
- Pricing: specific current tier pricing not confirmed in available sources for 2025, but historically structured as Free / Personal (paid) / Business tiers with monthly and annual billing (exact $ figures not verified in this research pass — flagged as not independently confirmed).
- Target customer: prosumers and small-to-mid-size teams who want a visually polished, document-centric alternative to Notion, spanning personal notes through team docs.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: a block-based, highly designed document/notes tool emphasizing visual polish, native performance, and flexible output (documents that can be formatted like a webpage, PDF, or long-form doc), positioned as more "beautiful" and native-feeling than typical web-first competitors.
- Platform availability: macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Windows (in development/rollout), Android (launched November 2025), and a full-featured web app at docs.craft.do.
- Key features: block-based editing (paragraphs, headings, images, tables, checklists, embeds all movable/stylable blocks), real-time sync across devices, offline support, and Craft Assistant (AI features) increasingly rolled out across platforms including the new Android app.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: estimated in the $1M–$10M range (per Getlatka/Craft.co aggregation of third-party estimates); not officially disclosed by the company.
- Funding: $20.8M total funding raised (per Crunchbase), including a $12M Series B round closed September 2022; investors include Creandum, InReach Ventures, EBRD Venture Capital, and angel investor Gareth Williams.
- Users/customers: not publicly disclosed at a specific figure.
- Growth trajectory: the 2025 Android launch (following a long iOS/Mac-first strategy) signals a deliberate expansion push into the larger cross-platform/Windows-and-Android market to compete more directly with Notion and Craft's broader note-taking peer set.

## Competitive Position
Craft competes as a design-and-native-performance-focused alternative to Notion, appealing to users who prioritize aesthetics and native app feel over Notion's more utilitarian, web-first, database-heavy approach. Its moat is brand/design reputation among Apple-ecosystem users, but its later, slower expansion to Windows and Android (only reaching Android in late 2025) has left it structurally behind Notion in platform reach and ecosystem breadth.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (freemium SaaS — Personal/Business tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** Business/team collaboration tier, Craft Assistant AI features increasingly gated or bundled into higher tiers.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion with a light sales touch for Business accounts — Apple ecosystem app-store visibility (Apple Design Award), design-community word of mouth, content/SEO, plus some paid-marketing spend typical of a VC-backed startup, and a 2025 Android launch aimed at broadening the acquisition funnel beyond Apple-only users.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $80–$220 (estimated). Sits above pure indie-freemium because Craft is VC-backed and likely runs paid-acquisition/content campaigns, but below full SMB-sales-led CAC since Business deals are still largely self-serve rather than direct-sales-driven.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $150–$350 (estimated) — e.g., Personal tier at roughly $5–8/mo × ~30-month avg retention ≈ $150–$240, with Business-tier seats and AI-feature attach pulling the blended figure toward the higher end.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~1.8:1 (estimated) — below the healthy 3:1 benchmark, consistent with Craft's relatively modest estimated $1M–$10M revenue against $20.8M in total funding raised, suggesting acquisition costs may currently be running ahead of what the business converts into durable revenue.
- **User demographics:** Inferred from positioning/platform: design-conscious prosumers and small-team professionals in the Apple ecosystem (macOS/iOS-first), likely 25–45, skewing toward US/Europe and creative/knowledge-work professions; no official demographic data disclosed.

- **Estimated user geography:** Global/Distributed with US/Europe skew, top countries: United States (~30%), Germany (~10%), United Kingdom (~8%), Hungary (~4%), France (~4%) — estimated from Apple-ecosystem platform reach (macOS/iOS-first, design-conscious prosumers concentrated in US/Western Europe) and HQ location (Budapest, Hungary), not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
