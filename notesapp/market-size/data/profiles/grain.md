# Grain

## Overview
- Independent, privately held, VC-backed startup.
- Founded 2018; headquartered in San Francisco, California (founders not fully confirmed in public sources beyond limited references).
- Employee count estimate: roughly 40-70 (estimated based on $20M total funding and mid-market SaaS scaling norms; not disclosed).

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription SaaS.
- Pricing: Free tier ($0); Starter $15/seat/month billed annually ($19 monthly); Business $29/seat/month billed annually ($39 monthly); Enterprise custom/quote-based. Source: claap.io/blog/grain-pricing, vendr.com/marketplace/grain.
- Target customer: originally revenue teams (sales coaching, deal collaboration); has broadened to product research, customer success, and cross-functional knowledge-sharing teams.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiators: strong emphasis on shareable video highlight clips and lightweight collaborative "story" building from meeting moments, rather than pure transcription/summary output.
- Platform availability: integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams; CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot) and Slack.
- Key features: clip-based highlight reels for sharing key moments across teams, AI meeting notes, and cross-functional workspace organization — positioning Grain as a knowledge-sharing/collaboration tool as much as a notetaker.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not disclosed; estimated in the low-to-mid single-digit millions to ~$10M ARR range (estimated, based on total funding raised and stage).
- Users/customers: not disclosed in available sources.
- Funding: $16M Series A led by Tiger Global (John Curtius), with participation from the Zoom Apps Fund, Unusual Ventures, and Freestyle VC; total raised approximately $20M. Source: grain.com/blog/grain-raised-series-a-funding, clay.com/dossier/grain-funding.
- Growth trajectory: expanding use cases beyond sales (product research, CX, cross-functional teams) as of 2026, suggesting an attempt to widen TAM amid intensifying competition from Granola and tl;dv.

## Competitive Position
- Grain differentiates through its video-clip-first, storytelling approach to meeting content rather than pure transcript/summary output, giving it a niche appeal for teams that want to share "voice of customer" moments internally.
- It lacks the scale of funding and buzz that Granola and Read AI have generated in 2025-2026, and its category (AI notetaking) has become commoditized with strong bundled competitors (Zoom AI Companion, Google Meet's native AI) eroding standalone demand.
- Its moat is weak on the core transcription/summarization layer; its differentiation rests on UX and workflow (clip curation) that could be replicated by larger, better-capitalized competitors.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Starter/Business/Enterprise tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** Enterprise custom-quote tier for larger cross-functional deployments; clip-sharing virality drives some inbound but no separate paid add-on stream; no ads.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — freemium signup with shareable video clips acting as a viral growth loop, plus light sales assist for Business/Enterprise accounts.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $50–$180 (estimated) — moderate blend given a mostly PLG/viral-clip-driven funnel with some outbound for cross-functional/enterprise expansion.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $300–$800 (estimated) — based on ~$15-29/seat/month blended ARPU and ~18-24 month average retention typical of this competitive, commoditizing category.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** roughly 4:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Originally sales teams, now broadening to product, CX, and cross-functional knowledge workers; SMB-to-mid-market; primarily US-based (San Francisco HQ).
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant, top countries: United States (~65%), Canada (~10%), United Kingdom (~8%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
