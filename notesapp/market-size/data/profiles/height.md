# Height

## Overview
- Was a private, VC-backed company. **Height ceased operations and shut down on September 24, 2025** — it was not acquired; the company discontinued the product entirely. This profile documents its history for reference.
- Founded in 2018; the product publicly launched and operated for roughly 3.5 years before shutdown. Headquartered in San Francisco.
- Employee count: not meaningfully trackable post-shutdown; the company was small (estimated single-digit to low-double-digit team based on funding size) prior to closure.

## Business Model
- Was a SaaS subscription, per-seat project management tool combining tasks, chat, and adaptive/automated workflows in one product ("autonomous project management").
- Specific tiered pricing at time of shutdown is not reliably documented in current sources (product and pricing pages no longer live). Historically positioned as a modern Asana/Linear competitor with collaborative, real-time task management.
- Target customer: startups and product/engineering teams, similar audience to Linear and Asana.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: positioned itself as an "autonomous" project management tool blending real-time collaborative docs, chat, and task management with AI-driven automation of routine PM work — an early entrant into AI-agent-assisted project management before the category became crowded.
- Platform availability: web and desktop app (at time of operation).
- Key features: adaptive workflows, integrated chat alongside tasks, and (later) AI features aimed at automating status updates and triage — conceptually similar to what Linear's "Linear Agent" and other competitors later built out more successfully.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed; company was pre-mass-scale at time of shutdown.
- Users/customers: not publicly disclosed at meaningful scale.
- Funding history: raised approximately $18.3M in total funding, with a $14M Series A in September 2021 as its most recent round. Source: [Dealroom — Height](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/height).
- Growth trajectory: the company's own shutdown announcement (framed as a difficult decision after 3.5 years) indicates it was unable to reach sustainable scale or a follow-on funding round in an increasingly crowded PM-tool and AI-agent market. Source: [Creativerly](https://www.creativerly.com/height-app-is-shutting-down/), [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/3/height-project-management-tool-to-shut-down-by-september-2025/).

## Competitive Position
Height is a cautionary example rather than an active competitor: it entered the crowded project-management space (against Linear, Asana, Basecamp, Monday.com, ClickUp) with a differentiated "autonomous PM" pitch but could not build a durable moat or reach scale before capital ran out, shutting down in September 2025. Its closure underscores how difficult it is for a VC-funded, feature-differentiated PM tool to compete against both well-capitalized incumbents and free/cheap alternatives without a clear wedge or network effect.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (specific tiered pricing not reliably documented post-shutdown; historically positioned similarly to Linear/Asana).
- **Secondary monetization:** AI-driven automation features (adaptive workflows, status-update automation) were being developed as differentiators but there is no evidence they became a distinct paid upsell tier before shutdown.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — targeted the same startup/product-engineering audience as Linear via product-led adoption, without the scale of content marketing or brand recognition its competitors built.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $400–$1,200 (estimated). Entering an already-crowded PM category against well-capitalized incumbents (Linear, Asana, Monday.com) without a distinct viral wedge likely pushed CAC toward the higher end of the self-serve PLG range — a plausible contributor to its inability to reach sustainable scale.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $1,500–$3,000 (estimated). Pre-shutdown, Height likely had shorter average customer lifetimes (elevated churn typical of a differentiated-but-not-defensible entrant competing against stronger incumbents) and modest ACV, well below what mature competitors like Linear achieve.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~2:1 (estimated) — an unhealthy ratio (below the ~3:1 minimum generally considered sustainable), consistent with Height's inability to reach scale or secure follow-on funding before shutting down in September 2025.
- **User demographics:** Startups and product/engineering teams, the same audience profile as Linear and Asana — company shutdown suggests it never achieved sufficient penetration or retention within that segment to sustain the business.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant, top countries: United States (~55%), United Kingdom (~9%), Canada (~7%), Germany (~5%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
