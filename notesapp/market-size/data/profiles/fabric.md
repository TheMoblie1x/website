# Fabric (AI personal knowledge app)

## Overview
- Private startup (fabric.so); note this is distinct from the unrelated open-source CLI project "danielmiessler/Fabric" and from Fabric Health (healthcare AI) — this profile covers the consumer/personal AI knowledge-workspace product at fabric.so.
- Based in New York; specific founding year and named founders are not consistently confirmed across available sources (one source lists 2026, which likely reflects a data-aggregator default rather than an accurate founding date — treat founding year as unconfirmed).
- Employee count estimate: very small/early-stage team, likely under 20 (estimated based on lack of disclosed large funding rounds and product maturity).

## Business Model
- Freemium/low-cost subscription SaaS: personal AI knowledge workspace that auto-organizes notes, files, web clips, and PDFs into a searchable knowledge graph without manual folder/tag maintenance.
- Pricing: sources vary — one cites a flat $5/month plan (positioned as below per-seat competitor pricing) with a free tier included; another cites a free plan plus "Premium" from $8/month. Treat exact current pricing as needing direct verification at fabric.so/pricing-and-plans-for-individuals given the discrepancy between sources.
- Target customer: individuals (students, founders, knowledge workers) wanting automatic organization of scattered personal information (notes, clippings, documents) without manual system-building; also markets tailored use cases (students, founders/startups).

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: AI auto-organization is the central mechanic — content is automatically tagged, labeled, and filed as it's added, removing the manual folder/tag-structure burden common to Notion/Evernote-style tools.
- Platform availability: Web app and mobile apps (iOS and Android, per Google Play listing "Fabric: your second brain"); no evidence of a self-hosted option (cloud-only consumer product).
- Key features: automatic content organization/tagging, AI personal assistant for querying saved knowledge, semantic search across the knowledge graph, use-case-tailored modes (e.g., AI tutor for students, founder-focused workspace).

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed.
- Adoption proxy: reported 300,000+ users with a 4.7-star average rating across platforms — a solid consumer-app adoption signal.
- Funding history: no specific funding rounds, amounts, or investors were found in available sources; funding status should be treated as undisclosed/unconfirmed rather than assumed to be zero.
- Growth trajectory: not quantified in available sources beyond the 300K+ user figure; no historical growth data found.

## Competitive Position
Fabric competes directly with Recall, Mem, and Notion's AI features in the "AI-organized second brain" category, differentiating on low-friction automatic organization and consumer-friendly flat pricing rather than the collaborative/team workspace angle of Notion or AppFlowy. Its moat is limited — auto-tagging and semantic search are increasingly standard LLM-wrapper features — so its competitive position depends heavily on UX polish, price positioning (undercutting per-seat tools), and its stated 300K+ user base as a distribution/retention advantage rather than defensible technology.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription — freemium consumer app with a free tier plus paid Basic (~$6/month) and Pro/Premium (~$16/month) tiers (pricing varies by source; treat as approximate).
- **Secondary monetization:** Use-case-tailored paid modes (e.g., student/founder-focused workspaces) layered on the same subscription tiers; no ads, no enterprise tier or API/data licensing disclosed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — a consumer app-store/web product with an undisclosed, apparently modest funding base, relying on organic discovery, App Store/Play Store presence, and word of mouth rather than a large paid-growth team.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $10-30 (estimated) — early-stage consumer freemium app with no disclosed large funding round, implying acquisition leans organic/App-Store-driven rather than paid-growth-team-scale spend.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $100-200 (estimated) — based on a ~$6-16/month blended price point at an assumed ~15 month average paid retention typical of a consumer AI productivity app.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Students, founders, and individual knowledge workers, roughly 20-35, with a US/New York skew given the company's base.
- **Estimated user geography:** US-centric with English-speaking global reach, top countries: United States (~50%), United Kingdom (~10%), Canada (~7%), India (~6%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
