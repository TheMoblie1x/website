# Read AI

## Overview
- Independent, privately held, VC-backed startup.
- Founded 2021 by David Shim, Rob Williams, and Elliott Waldron.
- Employee count estimate: roughly 50-100 (estimated based on $81M total funding raised and Series B stage scaling; not disclosed).

## Business Model
- Freemium subscription SaaS with tiered per-user pricing plus enterprise minimums.
- Pricing: Free (5 AI recordings/user); paid team plans starting around $7-15/user/month; Pro $19.75/month (or ~$15 annual); Enterprise $29.75/month (or ~$22.50 annual); Enterprise+ $39.75/month with a 10-user minimum. Example: a 50-person sales team on Enterprise pays roughly $13,500/year. Source: meetgeek.ai/blog/read-ai-pricing, checkthat.ai/brands/read-ai/pricing, read.ai/plans-pricing.
- Target customer: knowledge workers and sales/CS teams needing not just meeting summaries but broader workplace communication analytics (meetings, email, messaging).

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiators: goes beyond meeting transcription to score/analyze engagement and sentiment across meetings, emails, and messaging platforms — positioning itself as a "workplace intelligence" layer rather than a pure notetaker.
- Platform availability: integrates across video conferencing (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams) and extends into messaging/email analysis.
- Key features: real-time engagement/sentiment scoring during meetings, cross-channel communication insights (not limited to video calls), and AI-generated summaries/action items.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not disclosed; no reliable public estimate found.
- Users/customers: not precisely disclosed in available sources.
- Funding: $81M total raised, including a $50M Series B in October 2024 led by Smash Capital, valuing the company at approximately $450M. Source: search aggregation citing Read AI funding coverage (via meetgeek.ai/blog/read-ai-pricing and related trackers).
- Growth trajectory: well-capitalized relative to tl;dv, Grain, and Supernormal, though its ~$450M valuation trails Granola's $1.5B, suggesting slower momentum than the current category leader despite an earlier funding lead.

## Competitive Position
- Read AI differentiates via its broader "workplace intelligence" scope (engagement scoring, cross-channel analysis) rather than being purely a meeting notetaker, giving it a distinct wedge against single-purpose tools.
- Its moat is moderate: proprietary engagement/sentiment-scoring models are harder to replicate than plain transcription, but the category overall faces commoditization pressure from bundled offerings (Zoom AI Companion, Teams Premium, Google Workspace's native AI).
- Positioned as a mid-tier, well-funded competitor — ahead of smaller players on capital but behind Granola on valuation and buzz — making continued differentiation on analytics depth important to defend its position.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user SaaS subscription (Pro/Enterprise/Enterprise+ tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** Enterprise+ minimum-seat commitments; expansion into email/messaging analytics as a cross-sell within existing accounts; no ads.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — freemium trial (5 free recordings) funnels into paid tiers, with enterprise minimums (10-seat Enterprise+) indicating an added inside-sales layer for larger accounts.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $80–$250 (estimated) — well-funded ($81M raised) with visible marketing spend and a sales-assist motion for enterprise minimums, pushing CAC higher than lighter-touch PLG peers.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $400–$1,000 (estimated) — based on ~$15-30/user/month blended ARPU and ~24 month average retention given its broader "workplace intelligence" stickiness beyond meetings alone.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** roughly 4:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Knowledge workers and sales/CS teams needing cross-channel (meetings, email, messaging) analytics; SMB-to-enterprise; primarily US-based.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant, top countries: United States (~62%), Canada (~9%), United Kingdom (~8%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
