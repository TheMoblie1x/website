# NotePlan

## Overview
- Parent company: NotePlan (independent/small team), founded by Eduard Metzger — privately held, no institutional VC funding identified; appears bootstrapped.
- Founded: 2016.
- HQ: Berlin, Germany.
- Employees: Estimated very small team, likely under 10 (typical for an indie Markdown/PKM app of this scope; no official figure disclosed).

## Business Model
- Subscription-only (no permanent free tier, but offers a 7-day free trial).
- Pricing: Monthly plan at $9.99/month; Annual plan at $99.99/year (roughly 17% discount vs. monthly); some third-party aggregators cite variants as low as ~$4.99/month for older/promotional tiers — treat lower figures as historical or regional variation. All tiers include full feature access across devices (no feature-gated tiering).
- Target customer: Power users and productivity enthusiasts who combine calendar/day-planning with Markdown-based notes and task management — a PKM (personal knowledge management) and daily-planning niche audience, skewing professional/knowledge-worker rather than student.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: merges a calendar-based daily-note/planner structure (similar to Bullet Journal methodology) with Markdown note-taking and task management, rather than freeform handwriting.
- Platforms: macOS, iOS, iPadOS; no native Android or Windows app (Apple-ecosystem only), which is a meaningful limitation versus competitors.
- Key features: plain-text Markdown files (locally stored, iCloud-synced), calendar/daily-note integration, task rollover, linked notes/backlinks, templates, and scripting/plugin support for power users.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: Not disclosed; given its niche indie-app scale and subscription-only model, revenue is estimated in the low single-digit millions of dollars annually at most — this is an estimate based on team size and market niche, not a sourced figure.
- Users: No official user counts published; App Store category presence suggests a modest but loyal user base within the Markdown/PKM enthusiast community (a much smaller niche than mainstream handwriting apps).
- Funding: No VC funding rounds identified in Crunchbase/Tracxn-style databases; Tracxn lists it as "funded" in category terms but without disclosed round details — treat specific funding claims as unconfirmed.
- Growth trajectory: Steady incremental feature development (scripting/plugins, calendar sync improvements) typical of a sustainably-run indie SaaS product rather than a high-growth venture trajectory.

## Competitive Position
NotePlan occupies a specific niche at the intersection of calendar-based daily planning and Markdown note-taking/PKM tools (competing more with apps like Obsidian, Craft, and Sunsama than with handwriting apps like Goodnotes/Notability). It has no handwriting/stylus focus, so it's a partial fit for this "handwriting/student note-taking" competitive set — its moat is a loyal power-user community and calendar-native workflow, not scale or brand recognition, and its Apple-only, no-free-tier model caps mainstream growth.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (monthly or annual, no permanent free tier).
- **Secondary monetization:** None identified — no add-ons, enterprise tier, ads, or data licensing found; single-tier full-feature-access pricing.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion (via 7-day free trial rather than a permanent free tier) — driven by App Store search, word-of-mouth within the Markdown/PKM/productivity enthusiast community (Reddit, productivity blogs/YouTube), and no evidence of paid acquisition spend given the small team size.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $10–$40 (estimated). Indie freemium/organic band; likely toward the lower end given its niche, community-driven discovery and minimal marketing budget implied by team size (<10).
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $150–$350 (estimated), based on the $99.99/year annual price retained for roughly 2–3.5 years — power users in a sticky, workflow-embedded PKM niche typically show low churn once onboarded.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~10:1 (estimated). Very healthy, well above the 3:1 bar — consistent with a niche, high-retention subscription product with low marketing spend.
- **User demographics:** Productivity/PKM power users and knowledge workers who combine calendar-based daily planning with Markdown notes, roughly 25–45, skewing professional rather than student; Apple-ecosystem-only user base likely concentrated in North America and Europe (inferred from platform, pricing, and positioning rather than disclosed data).
- **Estimated user geography:** North America/Europe split, top countries: United States (~35%), Germany (~15%), United Kingdom (~10%) — estimated from HQ location (Berlin) / platform reach (Apple-ecosystem-only, English-language niche PKM positioning), not disclosed.
