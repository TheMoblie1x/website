# Guru

## Overview
- Privately held, VC-backed; independent company.
- Founded in 2013; headquartered in Philadelphia, PA.
- Employee count estimate: reported figures vary widely by source (from ~150-250 on LinkedIn/Owler to inflated multi-thousand figures on some data aggregators, likely conflating customer-side users); a reasonable estimate is 150-300 employees (estimated based on LinkedIn company size band and typical headcount for a company at Guru's disclosed revenue scale).

## Business Model
- Per-seat SaaS subscription, sold both self-serve and via enterprise sales motion; deeply integrated as a "knowledge-in-the-flow-of-work" layer inside Slack, Microsoft Teams, and browser extensions.
- Pricing (2025-2026): simplified to two tiers — Self-Serve at $25/seat/month (10-seat minimum, so effective starting price ~$250/month annual) and Enterprise (custom, sales-assisted). Earlier three-tier public pricing (Starter/Builder/Expert) has been consolidated. (Source: vendr.com, aggregated industry pricing pages.)
- Target customer: mid-market to enterprise, particularly customer-facing teams (support, sales, success) needing verified, trustworthy knowledge surfaced in-context rather than a passive wiki.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: "verification" workflows that flag knowledge as trusted/stale, plus AI-powered in-context knowledge delivery (browser extension, Slack/Teams cards) rather than requiring users to leave their workflow to search a wiki.
- Platform availability: web app, browser extension (Chrome, etc.), Slack and Microsoft Teams integrations, and AI-powered search/answer features (Guru AI).
- Key features: knowledge verification and freshness tracking, AI-assisted content suggestions and answers, deep contextual delivery into support/CRM tools (e.g., Salesforce, Zendesk), and knowledge analytics.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Disclosed (via industry tracker Latka): approximately $63M revenue in 2023.
- Funding history: total raised approximately $70.7M across multiple rounds, including a $25M Series B (December 2018) and a $30M Series C (April 2020) led by Accel, with participation from Emergence Capital, Thrive Capital, FirstMark Capital, Slack (Slack Fund), and Michael Dell's MSD Capital. Valuation not publicly disclosed.
- No funding rounds publicly reported since 2020, suggesting the company has been scaling on existing capital and revenue since then.
- Growth trajectory: reached ~$63M revenue by 2023 from a 2013 founding and ~$70.7M total raised, implying reasonably capital-efficient growth relative to peers, though growth rate post-2023 is not disclosed.

## Competitive Position
Guru is one of the more well-capitalized and enterprise-credible players in this set, with a differentiated "verified knowledge in the flow of work" positioning that sets it apart from passive wiki tools like Confluence or Notion, particularly for customer support and sales enablement use cases. Its moat comes from workflow integration depth (Slack, Teams, CRM/helpdesk tools) and its verification/trust layer, though it faces increasing competitive pressure from AI-native search layers and from Confluence/Notion adding similar AI-answer capabilities.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Self-Serve at $25/seat/month with a 10-seat minimum, plus custom-priced Enterprise).
- **Secondary monetization:** Enterprise-tier premium features, deep CRM/helpdesk integrations (Salesforce, Zendesk), and Guru AI capabilities bundled at higher price points.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) transitioning toward sales-led for Enterprise — a 10-seat-minimum self-serve entry point funnels smaller teams, while a dedicated enterprise sales org pursues larger customer-facing team deployments.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $2,000–$6,000 (estimated) — a mid-market/enterprise inside-sales motion with higher touch than pure self-serve peers, consistent with ~$70.7M raised and a sales cycle involving CRM/helpdesk integration work.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $12,000–$35,000 (estimated) — assuming ACV of roughly $3,000–$8,000+ (team sizes above the 10-seat minimum at $25/seat/month, more for Enterprise) and a 4–5 year average lifetime, supported by deep Slack/Teams/CRM workflow embedding that raises switching costs.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~5:1 (estimated) — healthy, consistent with a well-capitalized, enterprise-credible player that has scaled to ~$63M revenue (2023) without additional funding since 2020.
- **User demographics:** Mid-market to enterprise, with concentration in the 100–249, 1,000–4,999, and 10,000+ employee bands; primary buyers are customer-facing teams (sales enablement, support, customer success); US-heavy, with strength in tech/SaaS/digital marketing industries.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant, top countries: United States (~60%), United Kingdom (~10%), Canada (~8%), Australia (~6%) — estimated from disclosed US-heavy demographics and Philadelphia HQ, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
