# Fireflies.ai

## Overview
- Private, VC-backed company; reached unicorn status in 2025. No parent company.
- Founded in 2016 (product launched later); headquartered in San Francisco, California.
- Employee count: approximately 100-130 people as of 2025-2026 depending on source, including roughly 6 quota-carrying sales reps — a notably lean, high-revenue-per-employee organization. Source: [getlatka.com](https://getlatka.com/companies/firefliesai).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription (freemium), per-seat pricing with an AI-credit system layered on top for premium AI features.
- Pricing (2026): Free ($0, unlimited transcription, 400 minutes team storage, unlimited AI summaries, AskFred AI assistant); Pro (~$10/seat/month billed annually, or ~$18/month, 8,000 minutes per seat, 20 AI credits/month); Business (~$19/seat/month billed annually, or ~$29/month, unlimited storage, conversation intelligence, team analytics, 30 AI credits/month); Enterprise (~$39/seat/month, annual only, SSO/SCIM/audit logs/HIPAA compliance, dedicated account manager, 50 AI credits/month). Source: [Fireflies.ai blog](https://fireflies.ai/blog/fireflies-pricing-which-plan-is-right-for-you).
- Target customer: broad — from individual professionals on the free tier up through enterprise sales/CS/RevOps teams; reportedly used by 75% of Fortune 500 companies in some capacity.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: aggressive freemium land-and-expand strategy (generous free tier drove viral adoption) combined with deep integrations into CRM and revenue workflows (conversation intelligence, deal tracking) that go beyond basic transcription.
- Platform availability: web, browser extension, and bot-based integrations for Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and dial-in calls; extensive integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and 30+ other tools.
- Key features: automatic transcription and summarization, "AskFred" conversational AI assistant for querying meeting history, conversation/topic analytics, and CRM-native workflows aimed at sales and customer-success teams specifically (a sharper vertical wedge than pure general-purpose notetakers).

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: estimated at roughly $10.9M ARR (2024, aggregator estimate) — notably, this figure appears low relative to the company's $1B valuation, suggesting either the estimate is stale/understated or the valuation reflects strong growth expectations and profitability rather than current-revenue multiples. Company states it has been profitable since 2023 with sustained triple-digit YoY growth. Source: [getlatka.com](https://getlatka.com/companies/firefliesai).
- Users: reported more than 20 million users across over 500,000 organizations as of June 2025, including 75% of Fortune 500 companies (company-reported).
- Funding history: raised approximately $19.1M in total funding across 3 rounds, most recently a $14M Series A in 2021. In June 2025, the company completed a tender offer for early-employee liquidity that pushed its private valuation past $1B, achieving unicorn status without a traditional new primary funding round. Source: [Fireflies.ai valuation — Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/fireflies-reaches-1-billion-valuation-150000434.html), [whoisgrowing.com](https://whoisgrowing.com/p/firefliesai-why-it-broke-out-what).
- Growth trajectory: notably capital-efficient — reached unicorn valuation on under $20M of primary funding, driven by profitability and organic/freemium-led growth rather than heavy VC-fueled spend.

## Competitive Position
Fireflies.ai has become one of the highest-profile AI meeting-notetaker unicorns by combining a strong freemium funnel with deep CRM/revenue-workflow integration, differentiating it from more general-purpose competitors like Otter. Its moat is a large, sticky installed base plus vertical depth in sales/CS use cases, though it faces the same commoditization pressure as the rest of the category — bot-based meeting notetakers are becoming table stakes, and its long-term defensibility will depend on whether its CRM-native AI features (AskFred, deal intelligence) create genuine workflow lock-in.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-seat SaaS subscription (Free/Pro/Business/Enterprise tiers).
- **Secondary monetization:** AI-credit system layered on top of seat pricing (credits for premium AI features like AskFred), plus CRM-native conversation-intelligence add-ons (deal tracking, team analytics) that create a sharper vertical upsell than passive transcription alone.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — an intentionally generous free tier (unlimited transcription) drove viral, land-and-expand adoption to 20M+ users/500,000+ organizations, with a small quota-carrying sales team (~6 reps) layered on top to convert larger accounts to Business/Enterprise.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $25–$90 (estimated, blended). Fireflies' viral freemium funnel and lean, high-revenue-per-employee model (profitable since 2023 on under $20M raised) point to a low blended CAC typical of the meeting-AI freemium category, with the small direct sales team nudging the upper bound for enterprise conversions.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $200–$600 (estimated, blended), assuming an average paying seat at ~$10-29/month effective price retained roughly 2 years, with the CRM-native workflow depth (AskFred, deal intelligence) plausibly extending retention above pure-transcription competitors.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated) — healthy and consistent with the company's reported profitability and capital-efficient path to unicorn status.
- **User demographics:** Broad freemium base skewing toward sales, customer-success, and RevOps teams given its CRM-native integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot); reportedly used by 75% of Fortune 500 companies in some capacity, global reach.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-dominant with broad global freemium reach, top countries: United States (~50%), India (~8%), United Kingdom (~7%), Canada (~5%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
