# Bloomfire

## Overview
- Private company; VC-backed historically, with an acquisition/ownership change reported around February 2023 (specific acquirer not clearly disclosed in public records; likely a private-equity or growth-equity restructuring of existing investor stakes).
- Founded circa 2010; HQ in Austin, Texas.
- Employee count estimate: approximately 78 total employees (source: company data aggregators, 2026).

## Business Model
- SaaS subscription, enterprise-focused knowledge management platform sold via quote-based (non-public) pricing rather than self-serve.
- Pricing tiers: Not published; third-party estimates suggest most customers pay between roughly $12,000 and $48,000 annually, with larger enterprise deployments exceeding $75,000/year. Pricing model combines flat-rate and per-user components depending on tier — cite: Vendr/ITQlick marketplace estimates.
- Target customer: Mid-market and enterprise organizations (customer support, sales enablement, and internal knowledge-sharing teams), not individual developers or SMBs.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: AI-powered enterprise knowledge management and searchable knowledge base, positioned as a "collective knowledge engine" combining Q&A, content organization, and analytics on content engagement.
- Platform availability: Web-based SaaS with browser extensions and integrations (Slack, Salesforce, Zendesk, etc.).
- Key features: AI-assisted search and answer generation, content engagement analytics, community Q&A functionality, and integration with customer support/CRM stacks. Strengthened generative AI capabilities via its 2023 acquisition of conversational AI company Seva.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: Not publicly disclosed; no reliable third-party ARR estimate found.
- Customers: Not disclosed in available sources; historically served hundreds of mid-market/enterprise customers per earlier company marketing (not independently verified for 2026).
- Funding history: Historical investors include Austin Ventures, ESO Fund, Elsewhere Partners, Redpoint Ventures, and Silver Creek Ventures. A liquidity/ownership event was recorded around February 1, 2023, though the acquiring party is not clearly identified in public sources — flagged as uncertain.
- Growth trajectory: Company made an acquisition (Seva, July 2023) to add generative AI capabilities, suggesting continued investment and product expansion rather than decline, but no recent growth metrics are publicly available.

## Competitive Position
Bloomfire competes in the crowded enterprise knowledge-management space against Guru, Confluence, and increasingly AI-native entrants like Glean; its differentiation rests on content-engagement analytics and an AI layer bolted on via acquisition rather than built natively, which is a thinner moat than platforms built AI-first. Without disclosed revenue or growth figures, and given the ambiguity around its 2023 ownership change, its market position appears stable but not a clear leader against better-funded AI-native competitors.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Enterprise/team-wide contract SaaS, scope-based pricing (deployment breadth rather than pure per-seat) with custom quotes
- **Secondary monetization:** Add-on modules and expanded deployment scope (department-wide to company-wide rollouts); acquisition-driven feature bundling (e.g., Seva for generative-AI knowledge capabilities); no ads
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Sales-led enterprise — pricing page routes all visitors to a "Request a Price Estimate" form; no self-serve checkout, deals close through account executives and scoping calls.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $10,000–$25,000 (estimated) — enterprise sales-led motion with named-account outreach, demos, and multi-stakeholder procurement cycles typical of $12K–$75K+ annual contracts.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $75,000–$180,000 (estimated) — assumes ~$25,000 average annual contract value and a ~4–5 year enterprise customer lifetime.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated)
- **User demographics:** Mid-market to large enterprises; internal knowledge management, L&D, and customer support/success teams are primary buyers; US-centric with some global enterprise accounts.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America, top countries: United States (~78%), Canada (~7%), United Kingdom (~5%) — estimated from HQ location (Austin, TX) and enterprise customer base, not disclosed.
