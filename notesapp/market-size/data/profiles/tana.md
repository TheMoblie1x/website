# Tana

## Overview
- Privately held, VC-backed startup (Tana Oy / Tana Inc.); not owned by a parent company.
- Founded 2021; HQ in Oslo, Norway (with operations also described around Palo Alto in some investor listings). Founders: Tarjei Munthe Vassbotn, Olav Sindre Kriken, and Grim Iversen.
- Employee count estimate: ~11–50 employees (Tracxn range; some sources cite ~50 total).

## Business Model
- Freemium SaaS subscription with an AI-usage-credit dimension layered on top of tiered plans.
- Pricing (per tana.inc/pricing and aggregator sources): Free plan (500 AI credits/month, core editor features with limited integrations); Plus at $10/month (2,000 AI credits/month, Google Calendar sync, workspace sharing, full mobile/desktop support); Pro at $18/month (5,000 AI credits/month, unlimited workspaces, advanced integrations, AI model selection, password-protected publishing). 30-day free trial, no credit card required; 60-day refund window on annual plans.
- Target customers: knowledge workers and teams seeking an "AI-native" structured workspace — positioned between PKM enthusiasts and broader productivity/ops teams, with growing enterprise/team ambitions (per its "human-AI collaboration" framing).

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: combines a flexible outliner/graph note-taking model with structured data via "supertags" (a mechanism to turn free-text notes into structured, queryable database-like records) and deep AI integration for search, generation, and workflow automation — positioned as more structured/database-like than Roam or Logseq, less rigid than a traditional spreadsheet or Notion database.
- Platform availability: web app, desktop apps, and mobile apps.
- Key features: supertags for schema-like structuring of notes, AI chat/search across a user's workspace, voice-to-structured-note capture, and integrations (e.g., Google Calendar) — the "AI-native" framing and large pre-launch waitlist (160K+) were central to its public narrative.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed (early-stage company, product came out of closed beta/waitlist relatively recently).
- Users: reported a waitlist exceeding 160,000 users ahead of its public/funded launch (TechCrunch, Feb 2025) — a strong demand signal, though actual paying-customer counts are not disclosed.
- Funding history: raised a $14M Series A (announced Feb 2025) led by Tola Capital, with participation from Lightspeed Venture Partners, Northzone, Alliance VC, and firstminute capital; this brought total funding to $25M (including an earlier ~$11M seed round). Reported post-money valuation around $100M at the time of the Series A (TechCrunch/SiliconANGLE).
- Growth trajectory: the large waitlist plus a well-known VC syndicate (Lightspeed, Northzone) signals strong early demand and credible growth-stage backing; still in relatively early commercial scaling as of 2025–2026.

## Competitive Position
Tana is positioned as one of the more richly funded, "AI-native" entrants in the PKM/structured-notes space, differentiating through its supertags data-modeling layer and deep AI workflow integration rather than pure networked note-taking. Its moat is a combination of a strong VC-backed team, a large pre-launch demand signal (160K+ waitlist), and a genuinely distinct structured-data approach that straddles notes and lightweight databases (competing loosely with Notion and Coda as much as with Roam/Logseq). Its risk is intense competition from well-resourced incumbents (Notion) adding similar AI/structuring features, and the need to convert waitlist hype into durable paid retention.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user freemium subscription (Free/Plus $10/mo/Pro $18/mo, plus a higher Max tier at $80/mo) gated in part by AI-usage credits.
- **Secondary monetization:** Per-seat team pricing (first member free, then ~$3/member/month) and AI-credit-driven upsells (Max tier for heavier AI usage); no dedicated Enterprise tier yet, no ads, no data licensing disclosed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — built on a large pre-launch waitlist (160K+) generated through content/community buzz around its "AI-native" positioning, converting via a generous free tier and 30-day trial on paid plans; VC funding also supports some outbound/team-expansion sales motion as it targets broader team/enterprise use.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $100–$300 (estimated) — above a pure indie-freemium app given VC-funded marketing spend and AI infrastructure costs baked into the funnel, but still well below sales-led enterprise SaaS since there's no dedicated enterprise sales team yet; consistent with an early-stage, well-funded freemium product investing in growth.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $250–$700 (estimated), based on blended Plus/Pro pricing (~$10–18/month) retained for roughly 2–3 years among a moderately sticky knowledge-worker/PKM user base, with some uplift from AI-credit upsells to Max.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~2.4:1 (estimated) ($475 mid LTV / $200 mid CAC) — below the healthy >3:1 threshold, typical of an early-stage, still-scaling AI product where acquisition and AI-compute costs are elevated relative to nascent monetization; expected to improve as enterprise/team tiers mature.
- **User demographics:** Knowledge workers, PKM/"tools for thought" power users, and product/research professionals, roughly 25–45, with growing ambitions toward broader team/ops use; reviewer data skews small-business (software, consulting, broadcast media) with emerging mid-market/enterprise interest (logistics, medical devices, IT, retail, education). HQ in Oslo/Palo Alto suggests a Nordic + US-heavy early user base; inferred largely from review-platform segment data rather than disclosed figures.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-centric with strong Nordic/European base, top countries: United States (~40%), Norway (~12%), United Kingdom (~10%), Germany (~8%), Canada (~6%) — estimated from dual Oslo/Palo Alto HQ, Nordic + US venture investor base, and English-language PKM early-adopter reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
