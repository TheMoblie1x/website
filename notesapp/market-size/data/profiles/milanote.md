# Milanote

## Overview
- Privately held, bootstrapped and profitable — no venture capital beyond a small early angel round; not part of a larger parent company.
- Founded in 2016 by Ollie Campbell and Brett Warren in Melbourne, Australia. The product grew out of an internal tool built at the founders' own design agency before being spun out as a standalone SaaS.
- Employee count estimate: small team, roughly 10-25 people (estimated based on a bootstrapped SaaS of its revenue size and lack of large funding rounds).

## Business Model
- Freemium SaaS: a free tier with capped content (notes/images/links) funnels users into paid individual and team subscriptions.
- Pricing (per SaaSWorthy/Capterra, 2026): Individual "Pay per person" plan ~$9.99/month billed annually (~$12.50 billed monthly); Team plan flat-rate around $49/month for up to 5-50 users depending on source, scaling to ~$99/month for larger teams.
- Target customer: individual creatives (designers, writers, filmmakers) and small creative/marketing teams needing a visual moodboard/planning canvas rather than a structured project-management or database tool.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: a free-form visual corkboard/moodboard canvas (notes, images, links, files, to-do lists) rather than a linear document or database — positioned as a tool for the "messy middle" of creative projects (research and ideation), not final production.
- Platform availability: Web app, plus native Mac and Windows desktop apps and iOS/Android mobile apps; no dedicated self-hosted option (cloud-only).
- Key features: drag-and-drop visual boards, moodboards, hand-drawn style connecting lines, templates for creative workflows (mood boards, storyboards, content calendars), real-time collaboration.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: estimated ~$2.8M annual recurring revenue (Latka estimate, 2024/2025), disclosed as an estimate not an official figure.
- Adoption proxy: widely cited as used by millions of creative professionals; exact active-user counts are not publicly disclosed.
- Funding history: one angel round of ~$780K (some sources cite $593K) closed in April 2017; company has been bootstrapped/profitable since and has not raised further institutional funding.
- Growth trajectory: steady, low-key growth typical of a mature bootstrapped SaaS product; no signs of aggressive VC-fueled scaling or recent funding activity.

## Competitive Position
Milanote occupies a narrow but defensible niche as the visual-moodboard tool of choice for creative professionals, sitting between general whiteboard tools (Miro, FigJam) and structured note apps (Notion, Evernote) without directly competing on either axis. Its moat is thin — mostly brand recognition within design/creative communities and a simple, well-executed UX — rather than any technical or data lock-in, making it vulnerable to Miro/FigJam adding equivalent lightweight moodboard templates. As a profitable bootstrapped business it does not need to win the broader "second brain" category, just retain its loyal creative-professional base.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (Individual "Pay per person" plan, ~$9.99-12.50/month).
- **Secondary monetization:** Flat-rate Team plan (~$49-99/month scaling with seats); no ads, no data licensing, no API monetization disclosed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — a bootstrapped, profitable company relying on the free tier, word-of-mouth in creative/design communities, and SEO/content rather than paid growth teams or sales.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $20-40 (estimated) — indie freemium consumer SaaS with no VC-scale marketing spend, acquisition driven by organic search, referrals, and creative-community word of mouth.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $180-240 (estimated) — based on ~$9.99-12.50/month at an assumed ~18-24 month average paid retention for a sticky, habit-forming creative tool.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~6:1 (estimated).
- **User demographics:** Individual creative professionals (designers, filmmakers, writers, marketers) and small creative teams, roughly 25-45, with a skew toward US/UK/Australia given founder base and marketing language.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global with a strong Anglosphere skew, top countries: United States (~40%), United Kingdom (~15%), Australia (~10%) — estimated from HQ location / platform reach, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
