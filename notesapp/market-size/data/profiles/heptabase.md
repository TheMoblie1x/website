# Heptabase

## Overview
- Private, venture-backed (seed stage) startup; not open-source.
- Founded 2021; HQ Taipei, Taiwan (team distributed). Founders: Yu-An "Alan" Chan (CEO), Ethan Wu, and Yeefun Lin. Company went through Y Combinator's Winter 2022 batch.
- Employee count: small team, estimated under 20 employees (no official disclosure found; YC-stage startups of this age and revenue level typically run lean).

## Business Model
- Freemium SaaS: free tier plus paid subscription tiers unlocking AI features, larger storage, and advanced canvas/whiteboard capabilities.
- Pricing (from heptabase.com/pricing, Sept 2026): Pro $8.99/month, Premium $17.99/month, Premium+ $53.99/month, each with ~25% discount for annual billing (exact annual figures not shown on page).
- Target customer: students, researchers, PhD candidates, and "lifelong learners" doing deep, long-form knowledge work — explicitly not positioned as a general team-collaboration tool like Notion.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator is a visual, whiteboard-based "knowledge base" that combines infinite canvases, bidirectional linking, and card-based note atoms, aimed at helping users build structured understanding of complex topics rather than just capture fleeting notes.
- Platform availability: Web, macOS, Windows, and mobile apps (iOS/Android) with sync across devices.
- Key features: multi-canvas "Whiteboards" for visual thinking, journal-based daily notes, bidirectional linking between cards and canvases, and (in higher tiers) AI-assisted features. Its positioning leans on visual/spatial organization as the wedge versus outline- or document-first competitors (Obsidian, Notion, Roam).

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: CEO Alan Chan has publicly stated the company grew to roughly $1.2M in (likely ARR/cumulative) revenue over its first ~4 years, bootstrapped growth largely off subscription revenue (source: founder interview referenced via Extruct AI aggregation).
- Users/customers: not publicly disclosed; no official download or subscriber counts found.
- Funding history: raised approximately $2.2M total, including a ~$1.7M seed round (closed April 2022) from investors including Y Combinator, HOF Capital, and Kleiner Perkins.
- Growth trajectory: Heptabase has largely self-funded growth after its YC/seed raise rather than raising large follow-on rounds, consistent with a profitable-or-near-profitable, subscription-driven SaaS trajectory.

## Competitive Position
- Heptabase occupies a niche between Obsidian/Roam-style linked-note tools and full whiteboard apps (Miro, Milanote), positioning itself specifically for deep learning and research workflows rather than team collaboration.
- Its moat is thin relative to well-funded competitors (Notion, Obsidian's large plugin ecosystem) — the whiteboard+notes combination is differentiated but replicable, so retention likely depends on UX quality and a devoted academic/researcher community rather than structural lock-in.
- Small, disciplined team and modest funding suggest a durable lifestyle/niche SaaS business rather than a venture-scale outcome, unless it broadens beyond its current student/researcher niche.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (Pro/Premium/Premium+ tiers, $8.99-$53.99/month).
- **Secondary monetization:** Tiered upsells to higher subscription levels for AI features, larger storage, and advanced canvas capabilities; no enterprise contracts, ads, or data licensing disclosed.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium conversion — growth driven by content in academic/researcher/PKM communities (YouTube, note-taking forums, word of mouth among students and researchers) and free-tier trial conversion rather than paid advertising.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $15-$40 (estimated). Indie freemium consumer app relying on organic App Store/community discovery and content marketing rather than paid ads, consistent with its lean YC-stage team and modest funding.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $250-$450 (estimated). Based on a blended ~$15/month average tier price and an estimated 18-24 month retention typical of student/researcher users who churn around graduation or project completion.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~9:1 (estimated) — healthy (>3:1), consistent with a capital-efficient, largely organic-growth SaaS business.
- **User demographics:** Students, PhD candidates, researchers, and "lifelong learners," roughly age 20-35; inferred from explicit product positioning and pricing rather than disclosed data. No strong disclosed geography skew, though academic/research audiences suggest a global, English-speaking, education-heavy user base.
- **Estimated user geography:** Global/distributed with a US/English-speaking academic core, top countries: United States (~35%), Taiwan/broader Asia (~15%), United Kingdom (~10%) — estimated from founder/HQ base (Taipei, distributed team) and global academic/research positioning, not disclosed.
