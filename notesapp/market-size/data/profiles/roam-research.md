# Roam Research

## Overview
- Privately held, VC-backed startup; not owned by a parent company.
- Founded 2019 (concept/company work began earlier); HQ distributed/remote (San Francisco Bay Area origin). Founders: Conor White-Sullivan (CEO) and Joshua Brown.
- Employee count estimate: ~10 employees (small, lean team relative to its cultural footprint — Crunchbase/RocketReach estimate).

## Business Model
- Subscription SaaS, direct-to-consumer, no free tier (notably, unlike most competitors) — new users get a 31-day free trial before requiring payment.
- Pricing: Pro plan at $15/month (or $180/year); "Believer" plan at a one-time $500 for a 5-year license (~$8.33/month equivalent), aimed at early/loyal supporters and offering perks like early feature access and priority support.
- Target customers: PKM (personal knowledge management) enthusiasts, researchers, academics, and "tools for thought" power users — a highly engaged but niche prosumer audience rather than mainstream consumers or enterprises.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: pioneered the mainstream "networked note-taking" / bi-directional linking + block-based outliner model that spawned an entire category (often credited with popularizing "tools for thought" as a movement circa 2019–2020).
- Platform availability: web app (primary), with desktop and mobile apps; historically web-first with less polished native mobile experience compared to newer competitors.
- Key features: daily notes as the default entry point, bi-directional linking between blocks/pages, a global graph view of connected notes, block references/embeds — the reference implementation many later tools (Logseq, Tana, Obsidian) were built in response to or inspired by.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed — always label as "estimated")
- Revenue: not publicly disclosed. Given no free tier and a loyal paying user base since 2019, revenue is estimated in the low-to-mid single-digit millions USD annually (estimated based on subscription pricing and reported user/community size, not a disclosed figure).
- Users: exact current user counts not disclosed in available sources; Roam had significant early viral growth in 2020 within academic/PKM communities but growth has reportedly slowed as competitors (Obsidian, Logseq, Tana) captured mindshare with free tiers.
- Funding history: reported figures vary by source — Crunchbase/PitchBook cite a Series A of $30M (Nov 2022) with total funding around $40.6M, while other sources cite smaller figures (~$9–12M); January 2022 valuation reported at roughly $84–95M. The inconsistency across sources suggests figures should be treated cautiously.
- Growth trajectory: after strong early viral adoption (2019–2021), Roam's public growth narrative has cooled relative to free/freemium competitors that emerged after it; the company has kept a notably small team, suggesting a deliberate lean/profitable operating posture rather than pursuit of hypergrowth.

## Competitive Position
Roam Research created and named the modern networked/bi-directional-linking note-taking category but has struggled to retain category leadership against faster-iterating, better-funded, or free/open-source successors (Obsidian, Logseq, Tana, Notion). Its moat is brand/mindshare as the category originator and a loyal paying niche community, but the lack of a free tier and a small team have limited its ability to compete on features or growth against rivals offering generous free plans and faster AI feature integration. It risks being remembered as the category's pioneer rather than its long-term winner.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-user subscription (Pro plan at $15/month or $180/year), direct-to-consumer, with no free tier.
- **Secondary monetization:** "Believer" one-time $500 lifetime/5-year license functions as an upsell for loyal power users (front-loaded cash + reduced long-term churn risk); no ads, enterprise tier, or data licensing disclosed, though SaaSworthy review data shows meaningful small-business (72%) and enterprise (22%) usage suggesting some team/business upside not yet formalized into a dedicated tier.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Organic + freemium-adjacent conversion — despite no free tier, growth is driven by a 31-day free trial, strong word-of-mouth/community virality within academic and "tools for thought" circles (its 2020 viral moment), and content/SEO rather than paid ads.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $30–$80 (estimated) — higher than a typical freemium indie app because the no-free-tier model requires more convincing content/community marketing to drive trial signups, but still well below paid-ads-driven consumer subscription benchmarks given Roam's organic, community-led growth history.
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $250–$600 (estimated), based on $15/month retained for roughly 2–3.5 years among its loyal niche PKM-enthusiast base (a highly engaged but small community with above-average retention for a prosumer tool).
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~7.7:1 (estimated) ($425 mid LTV / $55 mid CAC), a healthy ratio reflecting Roam's low-cost organic/community acquisition against a loyal, higher-than-average-retention niche subscriber base.
- **User demographics:** PKM enthusiasts, academics/researchers, writers, journalists, and consultants, roughly 25–45, skewing toward English-speaking, US/Bay-Area-origin "tools for thought" communities. Disclosed via review-platform industry data (academia, writing, journalism, consulting, education) rather than company-reported figures.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-centric with English-speaking overlay, top countries: United States (~50%), United Kingdom (~12%), Canada (~8%), Germany (~6%) — estimated from US/Bay Area origin, English-only product, and "tools for thought" community reach concentrated in Anglophone academic/tech circles, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
