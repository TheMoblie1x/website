# TriliumNext

## Overview
- Pure open-source community project (no company); a community-driven fork/continuation of the original "Trilium Notes," maintained under the GitHub organization "TriliumNext" after original creator zadam (Adam) moved the project to maintenance mode and handed the repository to the community.
- Origin: Trilium Notes was originally created by zadam; after roughly two years of active solo development, it transitioned to maintenance mode, prompting long-time contributors/users to fork it as TriliumNext to continue active development. No formal company, incorporation, or HQ exists.
- Employee count: not applicable — an all-volunteer open-source maintainer/contributor community, not a company.

## Business Model
- No business model: entirely free, open-source (self-hosted), with no paid tiers, cloud product, or company behind it monetizing the software.
- Pricing: N/A — free software; users bear only their own hosting costs if self-hosting a server instance.
- Target customer: power users and technically inclined individuals wanting a deeply hierarchical, scriptable personal knowledge base, often as an Evernote/Roam Research alternative.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: a hierarchical note tree of unlimited depth where any note can be "cloned" to appear in multiple branches without duplicating content, combined with scripting capabilities and relation maps — a structurally different model from flat-notebook or block-based competitors.
- Platform availability: Self-hosted server plus desktop client (Electron-based, Windows/macOS/Linux); web access via self-hosted server; third-party/community mobile clients exist (e.g., "Trinote" for iOS) since there's no official mobile app.
- Key features: hierarchical/cloneable note tree, rich note editing with code blocks, relation maps, scripting/automation, end-to-end encrypted sync, self-hosted server option.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Revenue: none — not a commercial entity; no revenue to report.
- Adoption proxy: original Trilium reached over 22,000 GitHub stars before the fork; TriliumNext/Trilium (now consolidated as TriliumNext/Notes) has grown to roughly 38,000 stars as of September 2026, indicating successful continuation and growth of the community post-handover.
- Funding history: none — entirely volunteer/community-sustained; notably, the project has used third-party tools (e.g., Dosu) to help manage the revitalized open-source maintenance workload, but this is tooling support, not investment.
- Growth trajectory: star count grew from the original project's ~22K to ~38K under TriliumNext stewardship, suggesting the community takeover successfully reinvigorated development and adoption rather than causing fragmentation/decline.

## Competitive Position
TriliumNext is a notable example of a successful community-led open-source "rescue" of an abandoned project, competing in the power-user personal-knowledge-base niche against Obsidian, Logseq, and SiYuan on the strength of its unique hierarchical/cloning note model and scripting depth rather than polish or mobile support. Its moat is entirely reputational/community-based (continuity of a trusted tool) with no commercial backing, making its long-term sustainability dependent on volunteer maintainer bandwidth rather than any revenue engine.
