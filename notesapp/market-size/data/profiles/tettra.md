# Tettra

## Overview
- Acquired subsidiary: Tettra was acquired by GSoft (a Canadian software company, parent of Officevibe and other employee-experience products) in October 2023. No longer an independent company, though it continues to operate under the Tettra brand.
- Founded in 2015 (pivoted to a Slack-native wiki shortly after) by co-founders Nelson Joyce and Andy Cook; originally headquartered in the US (Boston-area roots).
- Employee count estimate: very small team, approximately 5-7 employees pre/post-acquisition (Tracxn/Crunchbase-derived estimates).

## Business Model
- Per-account/tiered SaaS subscription (not strictly per-seat at entry tiers) with three plans, deeply integrated with Slack as a primary interface.
- Pricing (2025-2026): Basic plan at $40/month (Slack integration, Q&A, page requests, Google Workspace integration); Scaling plan at $80/month (adds AI features, advanced permissions, automation, usage analytics, API access); Professional plan at approximately $7,200/year (adds SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning, dedicated account manager, onboarding support). (Source: aggregated via eesel.ai and spotsaas.com.)
- Target customer: small and mid-market teams, particularly those already Slack-centric, looking for a simple internal wiki tightly bound to Slack workflows.

## Product & Positioning
- Core differentiator: Slack-native design — Tettra was built around answering team questions directly inside Slack and reducing repetitive Q&A, rather than being a standalone destination wiki.
- Platform availability: web app plus deep, first-class Slack integration (and Microsoft Teams support); less emphasis on native mobile apps.
- Key features: Slack-integrated Q&A workflows, page verification/content freshness tracking, AI-assisted answers, and permission/integration features gated to higher tiers.

## Financial Profile (estimates where not disclosed)
- Disclosed (via industry tracker Latka): approximately $3.2M revenue and 548 customers in 2024.
- Funding history (pre-acquisition): raised a total of approximately $1.5-1.8M across two rounds from a small number of investors — a modest, non-hypergrowth funding profile consistent with its eventual acquisition rather than an IPO path.
- Acquisition terms (GSoft, October 2023) were not publicly disclosed.
- Growth trajectory: modest, steady revenue growth; the GSoft acquisition suggests a strategic buyer seeking to round out an employee-experience product suite rather than a distressed sale, though deal terms are undisclosed.

## Competitive Position
Tettra occupies a defensible but small niche as the Slack-native answer to internal Q&A and lightweight documentation, differentiated from broader wiki tools by its workflow-first (not document-first) design. Post-acquisition by GSoft, it likely benefits from cross-sell into GSoft's existing employee-experience customer base, but as a small, low-funding product it lacks the scale or platform breadth to compete head-on with Confluence, Notion, or Guru for larger enterprise deals.

## Unit Economics & Monetization

- **Primary revenue source:** Per-account tiered SaaS subscription (Basic/Scaling/Professional), not strictly per-seat.
- **Secondary monetization:** Professional-tier add-ons (SAML SSO, SCIM, dedicated account manager); post-acquisition, potential cross-sell into GSoft's broader employee-experience suite (e.g., Officevibe) represents an emerging secondary channel.
- **Growth/acquisition model:** Self-serve + inside sales (PLG) — discovery via the Slack App Directory and Slack-native positioning drives low-touch signups, with sales assistance for Professional-tier upsells.
- **Estimated CAC (customer acquisition cost):** $800–$2,500 (estimated) — a small-team SaaS with a sales-assisted upsell path for its higher tiers, but overall low-touch entry given its Slack-embedded discovery model and historically modest funding (~$1.5–1.8M raised pre-acquisition).
- **Estimated LTV (customer lifetime value):** $4,000–$10,000 (estimated) — assuming a blended ACV of roughly $1,000–$3,000 across the $40–$80/month tiers (with some Professional-tier accounts at ~$7,200/year) and a 4–5 year average lifetime, supported by Slack-embedded workflows that raise switching costs.
- **LTV:CAC ratio (implied):** ~4:1 (estimated) — healthy, likely improved post-acquisition as GSoft's existing distribution reduces incremental CAC.
- **User demographics:** Small and mid-market teams, roughly 10–250 users, heavily Slack-centric; primary buyers span Support, Success, HR, Operations, and Engineering teams at growing companies.
- **Estimated user geography:** North America-led, top countries: United States (~55%), Canada (~15%), United Kingdom (~9%), Australia (~6%) — estimated from original San Francisco HQ and Montreal-based GSoft's post-acquisition distribution, not disclosed.

*All figures in this section are estimates based on business-model archetype and publicly available pricing — none of these companies disclose actual CAC/LTV. Treat as directional only.*
